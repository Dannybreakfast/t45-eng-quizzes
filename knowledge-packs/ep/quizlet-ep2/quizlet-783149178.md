# T45 EP2 — Quizlet set 783149178 (verbatim)

Source: https://quizlet.com/783149178/t45-ep2-flash-cards/

## 1. Descend below _______ feet cabin aft. with an OBOGS Failure

10,000

## 2. If below 1500 AGL and below 180 knots

Eject

## 3. Electrical fire immediate action

GEN switch off

## 4. (2 questions) If put you get a SLATS caution light

Maintain a/s less than 200 KIAS

## 5. Smoke and fumes in cockpit

Altitude descend below 25K, AIR FLOW knob off

## 6. With directional control in question, short fleld arrestment:

Hook down, land 50 ft prior

## 7. Uncommanded roll or yaw when transitioning flaps/stats

Return flaps to previous position

## 8. OXYGEN warning light, system won't reset

OBOGS/ANTI-G switch off

## 9. Blown tire after touchdown

Rudder (counter swerve) and go around if able

## 10. GINA failure

All the above

## 11. Abnormal MFD

Blinking screen, invalid display, blank screen, stuck push button

## 12. With barometric altitude failure

Use radar alt if below 5K AGL, use cabin altimeter if unpressurized, and check PITOT Heat is on

## 13. Engine failure catapult

Eject

## 14. Immediate Airstart

Pull Emerg. Oxygen actuator, throttle off, GTS button, idle

## 15. C AUG light on you will lose:

ARI, SBI, yaw dampner, and rudder trim

## 16. GTS fire

Emergency engine shutdown

## 17. Cabin pressurization failure

Descend below 25K MSL

## 18. Compressor stall

IDLE, Neutralize, monitor

## 19. Blown tire on T/O roll and abort

Rudder (counter swerve), Anti-skid off

## 20. Blown tire, no arresting gear

flared, half flap

## 21. CANOPY light in flight

Airspeed below 200 knots

## 22. Multiple MFDs f°d up

Momentarily set Display Power switch to RESET

## 23. Total Electrical failure can't restore power

No flap approach and make an arrested landing

## 24. Windmill airstart parameters

Below 25K, 13%, N2 min 250 kts

## 25. Abort takeoff

Idle, extend, brakes as req, hook 1000 ft prior

## 26. Electrical failure

No load shedding (eliminates 3 out 4 answers)

## 27. Pitot static failure question

Standby VSI

## 28. Settling off catapult

Throttle check full forward, 24 AOA

## 29. Launch bar warning light after cat launch

Divert to shore, min. descent rate landing with arresting wires removed

## 30. Gen failure

Confirm engine operation, reset Gen switch

## 31. Wheel brake failure go around not possible

Brakes release, anti skid- off, attempt braking, hook down 1000 feet prior

## 32. AC INV light

Reset number 1 and 2 inverters

## 33. Hook light still illum after hook should be down

Hook is not fully extended

## 34. Anti-skid failure

Release brakes, anti-skid off, brakes as required

## 35. 28 VDC gen bus failure will not remove

Primary stabilator trim

## 36. Fire light in flight

Idle, instruments check, secondary-eject
