## 121
**Q:** If the FIRE light comes on and then goes out, the gouge says this means:
**A:** Firewire temperature dropped below 300°C (fire out) — or burn-through — Gouge p.3: 'if the fire light comes on then turns off = temp below 300 or burn through.' p.37: firewire must remain below 300°C for 45 sec for the light to go out. p.53 #113: check light/continuity.

## 122
**Q:** Per IC 21/43, the first step of CLEAR ENGINE PROCEDURE / ABNORMAL START / TP FIRE ON SHUTDOWN is:

## 123
**Q:** Engine fails to light off within 15 seconds after moving the throttle to IDLE. This is a:
**A:** Wet start — Quizlet EP1 #1 / gouge p.5 #1, p.62 (G); p.56 #168: wet start = FF rises normally but no EGT/RPM within 15 s of idle.

## 124
**Q:** RPM stagnates around 45% while EGT continues to rise during start. This is a:
**A:** Hung start — Gouge p.4 / p.56 #168: hung start = RPM stuck ~45%, EGT rising.

## 125
**Q:** Hot start (gouge definition):
**A:** FF and RPM normal, but EGT rapidly to 550°C or to 570°C for more than 10 seconds — Gouge p.56 #168. Ground start limit 550°C (570°C max for 10 s), p.37/p.56.

## 126
**Q:** Overspeed during engine start — first step:
**A:** Throttle – off (clear engine procedure) — Gouge p.59 #11, p.65 'OVERSPEED START – THROTTLE OFF'; matches IC 21/43 Clear Engine step 1.
