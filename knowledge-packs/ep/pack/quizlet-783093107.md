# T45C EP1 — Quizlet set 783093107 (verbatim)

Source: https://quizlet.com/783093107/t45c-ep1-flash-cards/

## 1. Engine fails to light off with in 15 sec from putting throttle to idle

Wet Start

## 2. Audible bang, Increase EGT, RPM lowers

Engine Stall

## 3. During descent passing 7000 MSL, ACCEL Caution light

Expect slower engine acceleration during an approach

## 4. HYD 2 pressure failure

RAT will maintain HYD 2 pressure at 2500-3000

## 5. Taxi clear of active, NWS malfunction

Aircraft would be steered by differential braking

## 6. If aborting with a blown tire, set ANTI-SKID switch ON

False

## 7. ECA 2 Caution light

Land as soon as practical and do not exceed 90% above 20K MSL, 95% below 20K MSL, do not exceed 600 deg EGT

## 8. Just flamed out, airspeed is 150 and at 1000' you should

Eject

## 9. Abort procedures

IDLE, BOARDS retract, BRAKES, HOOK (1000 ft. prior)

## 10. LP PUMP light in flight

Avoid abrupt throttle movements and set min for flight

## 11. What requires an immediate shutdown on deck w/out 2ndary indications

FIRE light illums

## 12. When flying @ 15K, TP HOT light

Throttle idle (min safe flight)

## 13. On cat stroke, aircraft settles

Throttle full, check 24 AOA, if settle continues Eject

## 14. Launch bar system is

Electrically controlled, mechanically extended, and hydraulically retracted

## 15. Engine failure on cat stroke and unable to eject

Ditch straight ahead

## 16. For HYD 1 or HYD 2 Failure 1st consideration

Reduce A/S below 300 KIAS/.6 MACH

## 17. After F PRESS light illuminates and checking engine switch on you should

Avoid negative G maneuvers

## 18. While cruising 23K you get FIRE light, with secondaries

EJECT (not prepare for controlled ejection)

## 19. NWS AUG system provides yaw rate feedback in high gain nose wheel steering

False (yeah we had no f'ng idea either)

## 20. 3-4 questions that deal with procedures for COMP Stall and EGT RPM Light. Know the first 3 steps in that procedure and that's 3-4 free questions.



## 21. If you get a HYD 2 failure

Monitor HYD 1 Pressure

## 22. Executing the Engine Fire Emergency Shutdown on the Ground state the procedures verbatim

(A,E,B,D,F, if different use PCLI) 1) Throttle OFF, 2) Engine Switch-OFF (both Cockpits), 3)
Fuel Shutoff - PULL, 4) BATT switches - OFF, 5) Emergency Egress

## 23. During ACM you get a HYD caution light, RAT caution light and then the HYD light extinguishes you should:

Reset HYD 2 (correct but not the first thing to do, Instructar took note & future test may be changed)

## 24. First step in the EGT/RPM warning light:

Neutral/IDLE

## 25. If NWS AUG goes on during taxi, you should:

Momentarily press NWS button.

## 26. GTS Fire on deck:

Emergency Shutdown
