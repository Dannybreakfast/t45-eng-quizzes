# Answer key

1. **C** — 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft MSL (FAR 91) to reduce midair collision hazards.
2. **B** — Class C/D: 200 KIAS within 4 nm, surface–2500 AGL.
3. **C** — 200 KIAS under Class B floors and in a VFR corridor transiting Class B.
4. **B** — Hold at max endurance; T-45 SOP 200 KIAS.
5. **B** — Navy: 230 KIAS all altitudes unless depicted; USAF is 310.
6. **C** — Civilian bands: 200 (0–6000), 230 (above 6000–14,000), 265 (above 14,000).
7. **C** — T-45 always 500 lb IFR; CNAF is greater of 10% or 20 min at 10,000 MSL max endurance.
8. **B** — 20 minutes of flight based on maximum endurance at 10,000 feet MSL.
9. **B** — 5 years flight experience, 2000 hours, 100 hours actual instrument time.
10. **B** — Precision: published but not less than 200-1/2; non-precision floor is 300-1.
11. **C** — ≥ circling mins for runway in use, or VFR 1000-3 if circling not authorized/exists.
12. **B** — 200 ft/nm until reaching a minimum IFR altitude (MEA/MSA etc.).
13. **B** — Runway heading until 400 ft above field elevation.
14. **C** — No obstacles penetrate the 40:1 Obstacle Identification Surface (OIS).
15. **B** — Non-standard ODP wx mins do not apply to USN/USMC; often for see-and-avoid.
16. **B** — ODP is the default in absence of radar vectors or SID; one ODP per runway.
17. **B** — Primary: IMC obstacle clearance; secondary: efficiency/comms.
18. **B** — Pilot NAV SID: NQI3 CRP on DD-1801 route.
19. **B** — SID cancelled unless controller says “expect to resume SID.”
20. **B** — Climb immediately to restated altitude unless restrictions remain; still follow path-over-the-ground.
21. **B** — DCT + fix in route; Remarks REQUEST RADAR DEPARTURE.
22. **B** — No identifier; file first route point; Remarks state intent to depart via VCOA.
23. **C** — RMK/REQUEST VFR CLIMB ON COURSE (to where you enter IFR enroute).
24. **B** — Descend via = lateral + vertical per published restrictions.
25. **B** — From over COT via COT R-037 to LEMIG INT; then SAT R-175 to ELKAY.
26. **B** — 200 HAT and 1/2 SM / 2400 RVR absolute; T-45 uses 200-1/2 or published, whichever higher.
27. **C** — Add (200−100)=100 to DA 150 → single-piloted DA 250.
28. **B** — May continue at pilot discretion if mins drop after commence.
29. **B** — >2 a/c IMC approaches not authorized; penetration of IMC to obtain VMC is OK.
30. **B** — Visual approach requires 1000 & 3; field or preceding traffic in sight.
31. **B** — Contact approach: only the pilot may request.
32. **B** — Reported 1 mile visibility; remain clear of clouds; reasonable expectation to continue.
33. **B** — Visual: IFR procedure in VMC, no MAP, does not cancel FP. Contact: is an IAP with MAP.
34. **B** — Immediate climb; initial turn toward landing runway; then to published missed approach course.
35. **B** — Maintain last assigned altitude until cleared for the approach and established on a published segment.
36. **B** — Squawk 7600; DRAFT = route not altitude clearance.
37. **C** — 18,000 if altimeter ≥29.92; FL 190 if <29.92 (any distance).
38. **B** — Strictly advisory; normal 3° (~300 ft/NM) from MDA when MAP would preclude a normal descent.
39. **B** — MON backs up lost GPS/GNSS; CONUS coverage ≥5,000 AGL; rarely >100 NM to a MON airport.
40. **B** — Verbal with Tower/Base Ops at military; FSS by any means at non-military.
