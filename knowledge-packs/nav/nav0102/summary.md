# Summary — NAV0102 Introduction to INAV and Voice Procedures

**Source:** tsharp.navsea e-learning screen recording (`source.mp4`, ~28s, silent)  
**Course title:** Introduction to INAV and Voice Procedures  
**Content type:** Slide OCR / watchVideo visual transcription (19 slides). No usable audio — content is visual only.

## Short summary
Overview of **ATC facility responsibilities** (Ground, Clearance Delivery, Tower, Departure, ARTCC, Approach, FSS), **IFR clearance elements and readback rules** (training vs real-world), **mandatory voice reports** (holding; level-off in RVSM), **lost communications** (squawk 7600, Guard, VMC land ASAP, IMC **AVEFAME** route/altitude/leave-clearance-limit), and **in-flight emergency communications** (squawk 7700; MAYDAY / PAN-PAN elements).

## Key takeaways
1. **Ground Control:** Taxi to/from duty runway; can obtain/relay IFR clearances if Clearance Delivery unavailable; weather info.
2. **Clearance Delivery:** No control function — solely delivers IFR clearances.
3. **Tower:** Runway traffic, takeoff/landing clearances, airborne traffic in control area; airport advisories if no ATIS.
4. **Departure Control:** Separates departures; sequences IFR and participating VFR; radar service / VFR assistance.
5. **ARTCC:** Central IFR clearance authority; routes, separation, NOTAMs, weather as required.
6. **Approach Control:** IFR from ARTCC handoff to tower; radar separation/sequencing; assists VFR; arrival info on initial contact.
7. **FSS:** Primarily civilian (military may use); freqs **122.2 / 255.4**; flight plans; ARTCC relay; lost aircraft; PIREPs.
8. **Clearance elements:** ID, limit, SID/departure instructions, route, altitude, departure freq, IFF, holding, other as required.
9. **Readback:** Training — all clearances verbatim. Real world — must read back runway assignment & hold-short; deviations from filed; refuse unacceptable clearances; precede with call sign.
10. **Mandatory reports (FIH §B):** Report entering/leaving holding except military terminal + radar contact + instrument training; report level at new altitude in **RVSM (FL290–FL410)**.
11. **Lost comms initial:** Squawk **7600**; try other freqs; Guard **243.0 / 121.5**; ATC uses Guard and NAVAID freqs.
12. **Lost-comms objective:** Avoid extended IFR in the ATC system in VMC; land as soon as practicable (not “any unsuitable/unauthorized field”).
13. **VMC lost comms:** Maintain VMC; VFR altitudes (E odd / W even+500); watch tower light signals; rock wings (day) / flash lights (night).
14. **IMC lost comms — AVEFAME:** Route **AVEF** (Assigned → Vectored → Expected → Filed); altitude highest of **AME** (Assigned / Minimum IFR / Expected); leave clearance limit per EFC or ETA (T/O+ETE) / arrival rules.
15. **WARNING:** Do not accept clearance to an intermediate point off filed route short of destination without expected further routing.
16. **Emergency:** Squawk **7700**; MAYDAY (distress) or PAN-PAN (urgency) plus station, ID/type, nature, weather, intentions, position/heading, altitude, fuel, souls on board, other useful info.
