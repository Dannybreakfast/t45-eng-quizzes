# NAV 0104 Approach Plates — Quiz

Answer key at the bottom.

## Question 1
Emergency Safe Altitude typically provides obstacle clearance out to:

A) 10 NM
B) 25 NM
C) 100 NM
D) 200 NM

## Question 2
In designated mountainous terrain, Emergency Safe Altitude clearance above obstacles is:

A) 500 ft
B) 1000 ft
C) 2000 ft
D) 3000 ft

## Question 3
A sequenced-flasher / strobe indication on ALS symbology is shown as:

A) A Trouble T only
B) A black circle with white lettering
C) A dot above the ALS code (e.g. A5)
D) An asterisk next to ATIS

## Question 4
For CNAF 3710 single-pilot rules, RVR should be no lower than about:

A) 12 RVR
B) 18 RVR
C) 24 RVR (≈ 1/2 mile)
D) 60 RVR

## Question 5
T-45 land-and-hold-short (LAHSO) authorization:

A) Authorized anytime LAHSO distance ≥ 5750 ft
B) Authorized only at civilian fields
C) Not really authorized for T-45s
D) Required for all Category C approaches

## Question 6
If a low plate has no Emergency Safe Altitude depicted, a substitute concept from the low IFR chart is:

A) MEA only
B) OROCA
C) Decision altitude
D) TCH

## Question 7
At the outer marker with a centered glide-slope needle, you should see approximately:

A) Field elevation
B) The published glide-slope altitude for that fix
C) MDA for the localizer approach
D) Pattern altitude

## Question 8
For single-pilot PAR planning under 3710, if published HAT is 100 ft you should plan approximately:

A) 50 ft HAT
B) 100 ft HAT unchanged
C) 200 ft HAT (add 100 ft) and raise DA accordingly
D) Circling MDA only

## Question 9
A displaced threshold may be used for:

A) Touchdown on landing
B) Takeoff roll and landing rollout, but not as the touchdown point
C) Taxi only
D) LAHSO exclusively

## Question 10
Typical plate frequency listing order starts with:

A) Tower then ATIS
B) Ground then Tower
C) ATIS, then Approach, Tower, Ground, Clearance Delivery
D) Clearance Delivery only

---

# Answer key

1. **C** — ESA ≈ 100 NM.
2. **C** — 2000 ft in designated mountainous terrain.
3. **C** — Dot above ALS code = sequenced flashers/strobes.
4. **C** — ≥ ~24 RVR / ½ mile for single-pilot 3710.
5. **C** — T-45s not really authorized for LAHSO.
6. **B** — Use OROCA when ESA absent on low plates.
7. **B** — Published GS altitude at the OM/FAF.
8. **C** — Add 100 ft to HAT/DA for single-pilot PAR.
9. **B** — Displaced threshold: rollout/takeoff yes, touchdown no.
10. **C** — ATIS → Approach → Tower → Ground → Clearance.
