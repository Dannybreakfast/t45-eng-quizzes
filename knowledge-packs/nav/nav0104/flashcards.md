# Flashcards — NAV 0104 Approach Plates

## 1
**Q:** Where can you get official PDF approach plates and IFR charts (high and low)?
**A:** FAA.gov; printed NGA High Approach Playbook East also used; SkyVector/ForeFlight ultimately pull from these sources.

## 2
**Q:** What does the Trouble T on an approach plate header indicate?
**A:** Takeoff issues / complications out of that airport (often lighting-related notes).

## 3
**Q:** What does a dot above an ALS code (e.g. A5) mean?
**A:** Sequence flashers / strobe lights associated with that approach lighting system.

## 4
**Q:** MSA vs Emergency Safe Altitude — radius and clearance?
**A:** MSA typically based on a ~25 NM circle about the defining navaid (quadrants). ESA out to 100 NM: 1000 ft obstacle clearance in flat terrain, 2000 ft in designated mountainous terrain.

## 5
**Q:** CNAF 3710 single-pilot RVR floor vs a plate showing 18 RVR (Atlantic City ILS example)?
**A:** 3710 requires no lower than ~24 RVR (½ mile); 18 RVR on the civil plate is not enough for single-pilot rules.

## 6
**Q:** If MALSR is OTS on an ILS that was designed for 1800 RVR, what correction is typical?
**A:** Increase RVR to 4000 (~¾ mile) per inoperative-components table / plate note (verify plate-specific notes).

## 7
**Q:** What is a displaced threshold usable for, and not usable for?
**A:** Usable for takeoff roll and landing rollout; not a legal touchdown point (pavement not hardened for landings).

## 8
**Q:** Are T-45s authorized for land-and-hold-short (LAHSO) operations?
**A:** No — T-45s are not really authorized for LAHSO.

## 9
**Q:** If a low approach plate omits Emergency Safe Altitude, what chart altitude can substitute conceptually?
**A:** OROCA (off-route obstruction clearance altitude) — same 1000/2000 ft clearance idea.

## 10
**Q:** How is pilot-controlled lighting depicted on plates?
**A:** Lighting symbology as a black circle with white lettering; click frequency to raise/lower intensity.

## 11
**Q:** At the outer marker on an ILS, centered glide slope should show what?
**A:** The published glide-slope altitude at that fix (e.g. 1433 MSL at Tuscaloosa example). Mismatch → consider localizer-only and time from FAF.

## 12
**Q:** Approx VSI for a 3° glide path at 120 kt ground speed (descent gradient table)?
**A:** About 637 feet per minute.

## 13
**Q:** For PAR minima under single-pilot 3710 rules, what adjustment is emphasized?
**A:** Add 100 ft to height-above-touchdown (and correspondingly to DA) so HAT is at least ~200 ft.

## 14
**Q:** Frequency order typically listed on the plate header?
**A:** ATIS, Approach Control, Tower, Ground, Clearance Delivery (plus ASR/PAR note if applicable).
