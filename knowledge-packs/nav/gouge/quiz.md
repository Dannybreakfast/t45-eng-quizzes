Answer key at the bottom.

---

# Answer key

1. **B** — Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed direct to the DP transition point.
2. **B** — 10%, but not less than 20 min max endurance at 10,000 MSL.
3. **C** — 200-1/2 when precision available.
4. **C** — Visual approach requires ≥1000-3.
5. **B** — Official Business Only cannot be IFR alternate.
6. **B** — Higher of last assigned or published MSA.
7. **C** — Missed approach for the original procedure.
8. **B** — Refuse and state reasons.
9. **C** — High NAVAID ≈ 130 NM.
10. **B** — MXF Channel 97.
11. **B** — Noncontinuous ATIS.
12. **B** — DPs encouraged, not mandatory on DD-175.
