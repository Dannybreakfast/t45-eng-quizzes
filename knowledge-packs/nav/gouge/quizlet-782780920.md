## 1
**Q:** Cleared for published DP on IFR flight plan. After takeoff "climb and maintain FL350..."
**A:** Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed via direct flight to the DP transition point

## 2
**Q:** On a flight from NAS Meridian to Montgomery Regional, prior to commencing a HI ILS approach, tune and ID NAVAIDS. Which TACAN should be tuned and IDed
**A:** Channel 97 (MXF)

## 3
**Q:** When flight planning, an airport designated "official business only"
**A:** Be designated as an alternate on an IFR flight plan

## 4
**Q:** For all flights in a jet, 3710 requires fuel from takeoff to landing plus a reserve of:
**A:** 10%, but not less than that required for 20 minutes flight at max endurance at 10,000 MSL

## 5
**Q:** Which of the following are true concerning HI-TACAN RWY 19L approach chart to NAS Meridian?
**A:** You must maintain 3000 ft between the 328 and 358 radials

## 6
**Q:** With all NAVAIDs and facilities operating, takeoff mins for a standard instrument pilot departing runway 9 at NAS Jax would be
**A:** 200-1/2

## 7
**Q:** When on an IFR flight plan and cleared to an intermediate point not in a filed route, short of filed destination, should not accept the clearance without first obtaining
**A:** Expect further routing

## 8
**Q:** On a missed approach at NAS Meridian you decide to go to your alternate, Maxwell AFB. Cat C mins for an ILS RWY 15 circle to land RWY 33 approach will be:
**A:** 500-1/2

## 9
**Q:** You are in the GCA box pattern, within 25 nm of the field in IMC, when comms are lost. From here, intercept a published instrument approach maintaining ____ until established on a segment of the approach
**A:** The highest of your last assigned or published MSA

## 10
**Q:** Your destination is forecasting 800-1/2 . Published approach procedures (NW winds) at alternate, Columbus AFB, lowest weather conditions for filing would be ____. ILS and ALS inop.
**A:** 700-2

## 11
**Q:** To be cleared for a visual approach, must report to approach control that you have field or interval in sight and weather must be reported as
**A:** 1000-3 or greater

## 12
**Q:** Inadvertent IMC and lose sight of the runway while circle to land
**A:** Execute missed approach for the original approach procedure

## 13
**Q:** Minimum forecast weather needed to plan for NAS Meridian (RWY 01L) as your destination? Maxwell AFB is alternate and forecasting 900-3. NAS Meridian NOTAMS: GCA O/S ALL, ALS O/S 01L.
**A:** 500-1-½

## 14
**Q:** A LOC transmitter has a range of ___ nm within 10* of centerline and ____ nm within 35* of centerline.

## 15
**Q:** Reading back an ATC clearance implies acceptance of the clearance unless ___

## 16

## 17

## 18
**Q:** ID the true statement concerning DPs and/or STARs:
**A:** DPs are designed to provide transition from the airfield to the appropriate enroute structure, and their use on the DD-175 is encouraged but not mandatory

## 19
**Q:** Yor MDA for the Maxwell AFB TACAN RWY 15 approach (with ALS) is published as

## 20
**Q:** Takeoff is allowed if the current weather at the departure airport is at least ___ and meets or exceeds the published precision mins for the runway in use
**A:** 200-1/2

## 21
**Q:** While on the Maxwell AFB TACAN RWY 15 approach, when would you execute a missed approach if you did not have the runway in sight?
**A:** At 1.5 DME from the TACAN

## 22
**Q:** Flying on J2 between Crestview and Seminole you would change your TACAN to Ch 122 when you are at the CEW 088/___

## 26
**Q:** Of the following situations, the one which does not require a voice report (NOT RVSM) is:

## 27
**Q:** You request a PAR approach to RWY 19L at NAS Meridian. Your DA in a single piloted aircraft for an approach to that runway would be:

## 28
**Q:** The theory and operating characteristics of a GPS system are:
**A:** GPS uses passive receivers and triangulation computations from signals received from at least 4 satellites to determine lat, long, and vertical position

## 29
**Q:** Enroute to NAS Meridian, you listen to ATIS, which gives airport info, but no weather info. You know that Meridian is ____
