## Short summary
Combined Navy Intermediate Navigation (INAV) exam gouge covering IFR flight planning, takeoff/approach/alternate weather mins, lost-comm / NORDO procedures, DP/STAR usage, NAVAID service volumes, approach-plate symbology, and Maxwell / Meridian / Jax scenario problems typical of the INAV written.

## Key takeaways
1. **DP clearance + "climb and maintain FL350":** Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed via **direct flight to the DP transition point** (do not remain on SID routing unless reinstated).
2. **Jet fuel reserve (3710):** Greater of **10%** of planned fuel **or 20 min** max endurance at **10,000 MSL** (distractors often use 20% / 10 min).
3. **Takeoff mins (precision available):** Typically **200-1/2** (PAR mins usable; not below 200-½).
4. **Visual approach:** Field or preceding aircraft in sight **and** weather **≥ 1000-3**.
5. **Official Business Only airfields:** May **not** be designated as an IFR alternate.
6. **NORDO in GCA pattern (≤25 NM, IMC):** Maintain the **higher of last assigned or published MSA** until established on a published approach segment.
7. **NORDO enroute (IMC):** Maintain **last assigned altitude**, but **no lower than MEA** for each route segment; squawk **7600**.
8. **Circle-to-land → inadvertent IMC:** Execute missed approach for the **original** approach, **not** the circling runway.
9. **Clearance readback:** Implies acceptance **unless** you first **refuse and state reasons**.
10. **High-altitude NAVAID interference-free range:** **130 NM**; **(L) VOR** service volume ≈ **18,000 ft AGL / 40 NM**.
11. **Montgomery HI ILS / TACAN:** Tune/ID **MXF Ch 97** for the arc / approach.
12. **ATIS with star (*):** Operates on a **noncontinuous** basis.
13. **Additional reports (not in radar contact):** Leaving FAF/OM inbound, estimate error >3 min, unforecast/hazardous weather — typically **"all of the above"** style answers.
14. **PAPI symbology:** Often denoted in IFR Supp lighting column (e.g. **"50"**).
15. **DPs on DD-175:** Encouraged but **not mandatory**.
