# Summary — NAV Gouge

**Sources:**
- PDF gouge excerpts (OCR): `drive-source.pdf` pages **11–13** and **78–82** (INAV / Timms-style NAV gouge)
- Quizlet set: [NAV Flashcards](https://quizlet.com/782780920/nav-flash-cards/) (set ID 782780920; canonical slug URL also resolves to 1154384596)

## Short summary
Combined Navy Intermediate Navigation (INAV) exam gouge covering IFR flight planning, takeoff/approach/alternate weather mins, lost-comm / NORDO procedures, DP/STAR usage, NAVAID service volumes, approach-plate symbology, and Maxwell / Meridian / Jax scenario problems typical of the INAV written.

## Key takeaways
1. **DP clearance + "climb and maintain FL350":** Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed via **direct flight to the DP transition point** (do not remain on SID routing unless reinstated).
2. **Jet fuel reserve (3710):** Greater of **10%** of planned fuel **or 20 min** max endurance at **10,000 MSL** (distractors often use 20% / 10 min).
3. **Takeoff mins (precision available):** Typically **200-1/2** (PAR mins usable; not below 200-½).
4. **Visual approach:** Field or preceding aircraft in sight **and** weather **≥ 1000-3**.
5. **Official Business Only airfields:** May **not** be designated as an IFR alternate.
6. **NORDO in GCA pattern (≤25 NM, IMC):** Maintain the **higher of last assigned or published MSA** until established on a published approach segment.
7. **NORDO enroute (IMC):** Maintain **last assigned altitude**, but **no lower than MEA** for each route segment; squawk **7600**.
8. **Circle-to-land → inadvertent IMC:** Execute missed approach for the **original** approach, **not** the circling runway.
9. **Clearance readback:** Implies acceptance **unless** you first **refuse and state reasons**.
10. **High-altitude NAVAID interference-free range:** **130 NM**; **(L) VOR** service volume ≈ **18,000 ft AGL / 40 NM**.
11. **Montgomery HI ILS / TACAN:** Tune/ID **MXF Ch 97** for the arc / approach.
12. **ATIS with star (*):** Operates on a **noncontinuous** basis.
13. **Additional reports (not in radar contact):** Leaving FAF/OM inbound, estimate error >3 min, unforecast/hazardous weather — typically **"all of the above"** style answers.
14. **PAPI symbology:** Often denoted in IFR Supp lighting column (e.g. **"50"**).
15. **DPs on DD-175:** Encouraged but **not mandatory**.

# Flashcards

## 1
**Q:** Cleared for published DP on IFR flight plan. After takeoff "climb and maintain FL350..."
**A:** Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed via direct flight to the DP transition point

## 2
**Q:** On a flight from NAS Meridian to Montgomery Regional, prior to commencing a HI ILS approach, tune and ID NAVAIDS. Which TACAN should be tuned and IDed
**A:** Channel 97 (MXF)

## 3
**Q:** When flight planning, an airport designated "official business only"
**A:** Be designated as an alternate on an IFR flight plan

## 4
**Q:** For all flights in a jet, 3710 requires fuel from takeoff to landing plus a reserve of:
**A:** 10%, but not less than that required for 20 minutes flight at max endurance at 10,000 MSL

## 5
**Q:** Which of the following are true concerning HI-TACAN RWY 19L approach chart to NAS Meridian?
**A:** You must maintain 3000 ft between the 328 and 358 radials

## 6
**Q:** With all NAVAIDs and facilities operating, takeoff mins for a standard instrument pilot departing runway 9 at NAS Jax would be
**A:** 200-1/2

## 7
**Q:** When on an IFR flight plan and cleared to an intermediate point not in a filed route, short of filed destination, should not accept the clearance without first obtaining
**A:** Expect further routing

## 8
**Q:** On a missed approach at NAS Meridian you decide to go to your alternate, Maxwell AFB. Cat C mins for an ILS RWY 15 circle to land RWY 33 approach will be:
**A:** 500-1/2

## 9
**Q:** You are in the GCA box pattern, within 25 nm of the field in IMC, when comms are lost. From here, intercept a published instrument approach maintaining ____ until established on a segment of the approach
**A:** The highest of your last assigned or published MSA

## 10
**Q:** Your destination is forecasting 800-1/2 . Published approach procedures (NW winds) at alternate, Columbus AFB, lowest weather conditions for filing would be ____. ILS and ALS inop.
**A:** 700-2

## 11
**Q:** To be cleared for a visual approach, must report to approach control that you have field or interval in sight and weather must be reported as
**A:** 1000-3 or greater

## 12
**Q:** Inadvertent IMC and lose sight of the runway while circle to land
**A:** Execute missed approach for the original approach procedure

## 13
**Q:** Minimum forecast weather needed to plan for NAS Meridian (RWY 01L) as your destination? Maxwell AFB is alternate and forecasting 900-3. NAS Meridian NOTAMS: GCA O/S ALL, ALS O/S 01L.
**A:** 500-1-½

## 14
**Q:** A LOC transmitter has a range of ___ nm within 10* of centerline and ____ nm within 35* of centerline.
**A:** 18, 10

## 15
**Q:** Reading back an ATC clearance implies acceptance of the clearance unless ___
**A:** You first state refusal and reasons

## 16
**Q:** Executing the ILS RWY 15 approach into Maxwell AFB, you should be at ___ AGL when crossing the runway threshold.
**A:** 50 feet

## 17
**Q:** When not in radar contact, the following additional reports should be made to ATC or FSS facilities without a specific ATC request (when not in radar contact):
**A:** All of the above

## 18
**Q:** ID the true statement concerning DPs and/or STARs:
**A:** DPs are designed to provide transition from the airfield to the appropriate enroute structure, and their use on the DD-175 is encouraged but not mandatory

## 19
**Q:** Yor MDA for the Maxwell AFB TACAN RWY 15 approach (with ALS) is published as
**A:** 500 ft MSL

## 20
**Q:** Takeoff is allowed if the current weather at the departure airport is at least ___ and meets or exceeds the published precision mins for the runway in use
**A:** 200-1/2

## 21
**Q:** While on the Maxwell AFB TACAN RWY 15 approach, when would you execute a missed approach if you did not have the runway in sight?
**A:** At 1.5 DME from the TACAN

## 22
**Q:** Flying on J2 between Crestview and Seminole you would change your TACAN to Ch 122 when you are at the CEW 088/___
**A:** 80 DME

## 23
**Q:** Select the choice that best describes the service volume characteristics of an (L) VOR navigational system. Range ______
**A:** Up to and including 18000 ft AGL out to 40 nm

## 24
**Q:** At Maxwell AFB, the PAPI system availability will be:
**A:** Denoted in the IFR Supp by a "50" under lighting

## 25
**Q:** The star next to the ATIS found in the upper left corner of the TACAN approaches to Maxwell AFB denotes that:
**A:** The ATIS operates on a noncontinuous basis

## 26
**Q:** Of the following situations, the one which does not require a voice report (NOT RVSM) is:
**A:** Reaching a newly assigned altitude

## 27
**Q:** You request a PAR approach to RWY 19L at NAS Meridian. Your DA in a single piloted aircraft for an approach to that runway would be:
**A:** 517 ft MSL

## 28
**Q:** The theory and operating characteristics of a GPS system are:
**A:** GPS uses passive receivers and triangulation computations from signals received from at least 4 satellites to determine lat, long, and vertical position

## 29
**Q:** Enroute to NAS Meridian, you listen to ATIS, which gives airport info, but no weather info. You know that Meridian is ____
**A:** VFR, with a ceiling of at least 6000 ft and better than 5 miles visibility

## 30
**Q:** Planning for a flight from NAS Jax to Birmingham, for filing purposes, based on the info above, what is the lowest weather mins required at Columbus?
**A:** 400-1

## 31
**Q:** When planning for a direct flight between two high NAVAIDs in the jet route structure, do not exceed the normal anticipated interference free reception range of each NAVAID which is ___
**A:** 130 NM

## 32
**Q:** Planning on a flight from NAS Jax to BAM
**A:** ETA 1500Z and alt of Columbus AFB

## 33
**Q:** Your filed destination is NMM with arrived weather best to be 300-1. The alternate field has PAR and TCN . The approach minimum weather at the alternate would be
**A:** TCN plus 300-1

## 34
**Q:** If you experience lost comms enroute in IMC, you should maintain:
**A:** The last assigned altitude but no lower than the MEA for each route segment

## 35

**A:** Squawk 7600, maintain VMC, and something traffic pattern and land at a suitable airport

**Q:** Which publication would provide information on preferred IFR routing?
**A:** FLIP AP/1

## 37

**A:** Idk thats all that was written

**A:** Indicate the portion of the DP routing that still applies and reinstated altitude restrictions

# Quiz

Answer key at the bottom.

## Question 1
Cleared for a published DP; after takeoff ATC says "climb and maintain FL350." You should:

A) Remain on SID routing and climb when convenient
B) Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed direct to the DP transition point
C) Level at 10,000 until further clearance
D) Cancel IFR and climb VFR

## Question 2
Jet fuel reserve required by 3710 (takeoff to landing plus reserve) is:

A) 20% of planned fuel or 10 min at max endurance
B) 10% of planned fuel, but not less than 20 min max endurance at 10,000 MSL
C) Fixed 45 minutes holding fuel only
D) 5% of planned fuel at cruise altitude

## Question 3
With precision approach available for the duty runway, typical takeoff minimums for a standard instrument-rated pilot are:

A) 0-0
B) 300-1
C) 200-1/2
D) 500-1

## Question 4
To be cleared for a visual approach you must have the field (or preceding aircraft) in sight and weather at least:

A) 500-1
B) 700-2
C) 1000-3
D) 3000-5

## Question 5
An airport designated "Official Business Only":

A) May be used freely as destination or alternate
B) May not be designated as an alternate on an IFR flight plan
C) Is restricted to VFR only
D) Requires special PAR clearance

## Question 6
In the GCA box pattern within 25 NM of the field in IMC with lost communications, maintain:

A) 2000 ft AGL until landing
B) The highest of your last assigned altitude or published MSA until established on a published approach segment
C) MEA for the filed route only
D) Pattern altitude only

## Question 7
If you lose the runway in IMC while circling to land, you should:

A) Continue circling to the intended runway
B) Climb straight ahead to pattern altitude
C) Execute the missed approach for the original approach procedure
D) Squawk 7700 and land any runway

## Question 8
Reading back an ATC clearance implies acceptance unless you:

A) File a NASA form later
B) First state refusal and the reasons
C) Ask for a phone number after landing
D) Remain silent for 30 seconds

## Question 9
Normal anticipated interference-free reception range for a high-altitude NAVAID (jet route structure) is:

A) 40 NM
B) 80 NM
C) 130 NM
D) 200 NM

## Question 10
For the HI ILS into Montgomery Regional, which TACAN should be tuned and identified?

A) NMM Ch 45
B) MXF Ch 97
C) CEW Ch 122
D) MGM Ch 40

## Question 11
A star (*) next to the ATIS frequency on an approach plate indicates:

A) ATIS is UHF only
B) ATIS operates on a noncontinuous basis
C) ATIS is inoperative
D) ATIS requires a discrete squawk

## Question 12
DPs on the DD-175 are:

A) Mandatory for all IFR departures
B) Encouraged but not mandatory
C) Prohibited in the jet route structure
D) Only used for lost-comm planning

---

# Answer key

1. **B** — Climb immediately to FL350 and proceed direct to the DP transition point.
2. **B** — 10%, but not less than 20 min max endurance at 10,000 MSL.
3. **C** — 200-1/2 when precision available.
4. **C** — Visual approach requires ≥1000-3.
5. **B** — Official Business Only cannot be IFR alternate.
6. **B** — Higher of last assigned or published MSA.
7. **C** — Missed approach for the original procedure.
8. **B** — Refuse and state reasons.
9. **C** — High NAVAID ≈ 130 NM.
10. **B** — MXF Channel 97.
11. **B** — Noncontinuous ATIS.
12. **B** — DPs encouraged, not mandatory on DD-175.
