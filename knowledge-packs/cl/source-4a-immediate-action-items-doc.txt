1. Throttle – OFF

ENGINE FAILURE 
If below 1,500 feet AGL and airspeed below 180 KIAS: 
1. Eject 
Otherwise: 
2. Execute AIRSTART 
AIRSTART 
1. Emergency oxygen green ring(s) – PULL 2. Throttle – OFF 
Simultaneously perform steps *3. and *4. 
3. GTS start button – PRESS 
4. Throttle – IDLE 
If unsuccessful (no relight within 30 seconds after moving throttle to idle or stabilized EGT exceeds limits): 5. Throttle – OFF (allow 30 seconds to drain if practical) If above 13% RPM and 250 KIAS, repeat steps *3 and *4. Otherwise, proceed to step 6. 
COMPRESSOR STALL OR EGT/RPM WARNING LIGHT 1. Throttle ­ IDLE 
2. EGT/RPM ­ CHECK 
If EGT is greater than 450°C for more than 6 seconds at idle: 
3. Execute Engine Failure procedure 
If EGT responds normally: 
4. Throttle – Slowly advance to minimum for safe flight 5. Minimize throttle movements 
ABORT 
1. Throttle – IDLE 
2. Speed brakes – EXTEND 
3. Brakes – APPLY 
4. Hook – DOWN (IF REQUIRED) 
5. BRAKES – Release prior to crossing the arresting gear 
EMERGENCY CATAPULT FLYAWAY 
1. Throttle ­ MRT 
2. Maintain 24 units AOA 
If engine failed, or unable to stop settle: 
3. Eject

4. BRAKES – Apply Gradually
