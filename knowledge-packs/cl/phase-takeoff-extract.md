# 4. Tower, Hold Short, Runup & Takeoff

## Doc
Ch 3 Pri: “Kingsville Tower, Blazer 201, takeoff”
“Blazer 201, wind 130 at 15, runway 13R cleared for takeoff, change to departure”
“Cleared for takeoff runway 13R, switching, Blazer 201”
Change to channel 4 on PRI.
Prior to crossing hold short clear runway and short final and state:
“Groove is Clear”
Upon receiving t/o clearance, begin hold short checks. These will be conducted on the roll while taking the runway. If you are not given a takeoff clearance, you can begin your hold short checks, but do not turn on pitot heat until you are cleared for takeoff.
Hold Short Checklist:
1. Fuel Control - Normal
2. Pitot Heat - On
3. Strobes - On
4. Landing/Taxi Light - On
5. IFF - Norm
6. AV BITS - Checked
                RMFD: Menu-BIT, verify there are no degradations. Set MFD back to HSI.
7. “Fuel Control Normal, Pitot Heat on, Lights on, IFF XXXX Norm, No AV BITS, Winds are left to right”
        R/C: “Fuel Control Normal”
Runup: Once you are lined up on runway centerline, come to a complete stop and conduct the following
1. “Good headings, no off flags, watch your knees”
        R/C: “Clear”
                Verify Standby heading, MFD and HUD headings are in agreement and match runway heading. STBY Instruments - Verify no off flags
2. Brakes - Hold
3. Throttle - MRT
                Done with purpose to ensure there will be no problems rapidly moving throttle to MRT in flight.
4. Flight Controls - Wipeout (Same wipeout as described above during PC checks)
                Done with purpose to ensure your flight controls will operate appropriately. Verify hyds 3x3 during your wipeout.
5. EGT, RPM, FF - Verify within limits
                EGT at or below 610 deg C, RPM at or below 104%, Minimum RPM within 7 seconds is known before flight and is based on temperature (usually 97% in Texas and it is found on the IFG checklists), No specific number for FF but it should be high since you are at MRT.
6. Batt Voltage - Verify between 27-29 volts
7. “Good EGT, RPM, Fuel Flow, No warnings or cautions, Hyds 3x3, Good Volts, Time is +02, Ready in Front”
        R/C: “Ready in Back”
Release brakes and begin takeoff.
“Airspeed Alive”
At 100 knots announce high speed. This signifies that if you need to abort it will be a high speed abort (high speed abort criteria in SOP). Shortly after, verify and announce line speed (you will calculate this for your kneeboard, it should generally occur at or before 1500’ down the runway and indicates that your a/c is accelerating properly). These two calls occur closely together so you can combine them if applicable. Do not always use arresting gear as the mark for good line speed. A better strategy is to approximate halfway between the boards for 1500’ or intentionally say to yourself whether the arresting gear is at 1500’ or not.
“High Speed, Good Line”
Rotate at 120 KIAS to a takeoff attitude of approximately 10 degrees nose high and let the a/c fly itself off the deck. Maintain this pitch attitude, being careful not to over-rotate past on-speed AOA. When safely airborne and above 140 KIAS, call to raise the gear and flaps.
“140, gear, flaps”
R/C: “Clear”
When cleared, raise gear and flaps. Small increase in pitch may be needed when flaps/slats come up. Verify gear and flaps are raised prior to 200 KIAS. Approximately 15 degrees nose high will keep you from overspeeding if needed. When gear is raised, verify the gear handle won’t budge by applying light force (less than 6 lbs) directly down (do not pull out). Once complete report
“Clean Below 200, Good Handle”
R/C: “Good Handle”
Accelerate to climb speed at an attitude of 10 degrees. Five knots prior to desired climb speed, increase nose attitude to approximately 15-20 degrees to intercept and maintain climb speed. Climb at 250 KIAS. Once safely airborne with gear and flaps raised, contact departure on ch 4 pri.
