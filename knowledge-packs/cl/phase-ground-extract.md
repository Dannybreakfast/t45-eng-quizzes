# 1. Ground — Prestart & Engine Start

## Doc
Prior to Getting In Cockpit:
1. Ejection Seat - Safe
2. Standby Attitude Indicator - Pull out and lock in the caged position before getting in the jet. This prevents wear to the device when power is applied.
Pre Start Checklist:
1. Plug in Comm/Oxygen hose and Anti-G on left side.
2. Batt Switches - On
                Check Each battery for 24-29 volts. Leave both on. Go batt 1 on, then turn batt 2 on and then batt 1 off to check batt 2, then batt 1 back on. This ensures there's no unnecessary interruptions of power.
3. Pri Radio - On, Channel 30 (ATIS)
                When done listening, switch to ch 29, turn off, put altimeter setting in
4. Remove ejection seat pin and MDC firing handle pin while listening to ATIS and stow in little storage compartment on right side.
5. Put on right kneeboard and write down necessary ATIS info (ATIS code, altimeter, runway in use, maybe winds)
Interior Check: (Do a sweep of the cockpit for these things when listening to ATIS)
1. OBOGS Flow Selector - Off
2. OBOGS/ANTI-G - Off
3. Ignition - Normal
4. Fuel Shutoff Handle - Up
5. Engine Switch - On
6. Fuel Control Switch - Normal
7. Contr Aug Switch - SBI
8. Throttle - Off
9. Flaps - Up
10. Anti-Skid Switch - On
11.  Emergency Landing Gear Handle - Check Stowed
12.  Parking Brake Handle - Set
13.  Exterior Lights - Down, Down, Down, Middle, Middle, Middle, throttle pinky switch middle
14.  Gen Switch - On
15.  Airflow Knob - Normal
        Everything else on right side should be off
6. Adjust rudder pedals and seat height when able. Rudder pedals as far back as you can and still get full throw, seat height adjusted to see both headings and AOB indications in HUD.
7. Check - Antiskid on (w light), flaps up (no lights), engine switch on, fuel control switch norm.
                “ICS check, switches set, Norm, Fuel __”
                R/C: “Loud and Clear, switches set, Norm, Both, Fuel __”
8. Warnings/Cautions/Advisories - 441 (Oxygen, Generator, Oil Press, Hyd Fail; Hyd, Canopy, LP Pump, F Pres, (NWS Aug Possible); Skid
9. “Lights and Tones”
                Hold lights with right hand, check AOA with left hand. Check all lights then flick back for 4 tones (Warnings, Cautions, Wheels, Weapons) (Don’t know if it’s in that order)
10. Paddle Switch - Press (NWS Caution comes on)
11. Hyds - 0, 0, 1250 (min)
Engine Start:
1. Verify throttle is off
                “Throttle off, GTS”
                R/C: “Clear”
                After saying “throttle off, GTS”, place fuel shutoff handle down
2. Signal Lineman for GTS Start (Shake left fist at lineman)
3. GTS Button - Press momentarily
                Start counting while looking at RPM Gauge and waiting for GTS Light (Should come on within 20 seconds). This is when I start to put on my lower garters. Usually I can get the right upper and right lower on before GTS advisory light comes on. Be very careful not to bump throttle and continue to pay attention to the start if you decide to do this.
4. When you have GTS advisory: Signal lineman for start (Two fingers with left hand)
5. Engine Switch - Start
                Start counting while looking at RPM Gauge and looking for ready light (Should be within 15 seconds). Ready light means that N1 is spinning in correct direction and has reached 100 RPM. Sometimes I start to work on the left upper garter as I watch for this. Again be very careful not to bump the throttle. You can scan fuel flow to make sure it is still at 0. Once ready light is on within 15 seconds and rpm is getting close to 20% devote full attention to the start.
6. At 17-20% RPM: Throttle - Idle (Go forward just to the first click)
                Now looking for fuel flow to come up and EGT light off to occur within 15 seconds. Monitor the start and make sure EGT doesn’t rise too fast. Looking for 45% RPM for everything to come on. (GTS and Ready light will extinguish). Look for normal start “light show”. Oil press warning light should go off around 20% rpm (Look For It to go off). Oxygen light won’t be off until obogs switch is turned on. Generator light should go off around 45%.
7. Everything Comes on at 45% - Two Hands
                Turn on HUD, OBOGS/Anti-G, MFD’s, Radios (turn aux to ch 1 (base)) (Put pri in tr&g and aux in t&r), IFF, TACAN (Set freq - 125x), NAV (Set freq - 110.9 (ILS for 13R))
8. Note EGT, RPM, and Voltage
                RPM Should stabilize at approximately 52% within 30 seconds of selecting idle. Max EGT for ground start is 550 deg with a 20 deg overshoot allowed for 20 seconds. Max EGT at idle is 450 deg. EGT will normally not rise above 420 deg during ground start. Voltmeter should read 27-29 volts. Make a note of EGT so that you know what it was before you run up to 70% and pull it back to look and see if there was a 50 deg C drop from this value.
