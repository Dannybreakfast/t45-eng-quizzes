# 3. Taxi, Final Checker, Marshall & Takeoff Checklist

## Doc
Taxi/Instrument Checklist: Conducted on the roll on the way to final checker
1. Parking Brake - Release
                Follow lineman. Get enough momentum where you can tap brakes to check them but still keep rolling. You just want the front to kind of dip down.
2. Hold NWS button with left hand for hi gain to turn and salute lineman with your right hand.
                Note: Do not use more than 75% power in the line and idle power in turns. No more than 10 knots GS in the line and 20 knots GS on the taxiways.
3. Flight Instrument Checks (Probably already done during plane captain checks. All I need to do now on the roll is check that the HUD/MFD HDG and STBY HDG match and then say the bold words for taxi checks.)
   1. STBY A/S - Zero
   2. STBY VSI - Zero +/- 100
   3. STBY Needle/Ball - Match MFD, Check turn needle in turn
   4. STBY Altimeter - FE +/- 75’
   5. STBY Attitude - Erect (Pull out to cage and then push back in)
   6. STBY AOA Indicator - Check for no off flag
   7. HUD/MFD HDG and STBY HDG - Match
   8. HUD/MFD Altimeter - FE +/- 10’
4. GINA - HYBD Boxed
5. “Altimeter Set __.__, indicating ___ on standby, ___ on glass. VOR Set ___.__, TACAN Set ___, IFF Set XXXX in Standby, Taxi Checks Complete”
Above is a picture showing the taxi flow. Final checker is the blue arrow pointed to the right and the first person in marshall is the green arrow pointed to the right above the hashed out area. The first four marhal lines past the end of the final checkers apes are the hashed out area. Do not stop to marshal here. Wait for the next set of four spots on the right and pick one of those. Taxi speeds are a maximum of 10 knots groundspeed in the line and 20 knots groundspeed outside of the line. Do not use hi-gain NWS above 10 knots.
Final Checker:
Set your parking brake. Do this anytime you are stopped anywhere other than the runway. While final checker is doing walk around have hands above rail. Do the takeoff checklist in your head now so that in marshall you can just say the words without having to check everything first (If you do the takeoff checklist in your head and notice that the pitch trim is not in the right spot do not reach down and fix it. Someone is walking around your jet. Fix it in marshal).
1. Checker comes around and lets you go.
2. Parking Brake - Off
3. Hold NWS button with left hand for hi gain to turn and salute lineman with your right hand. Heading to marshall.
Marshall Area:
Marshall area is further down to the left out of final checkers. The sim shows jets parked right in front of final checkers. You do not want to pull into these spots because your tailpipe will be pointed at the final checkers. Go down to the left away from final checkers like discussed above.
Use this area to finish up what you weren’t able to beforehand. This is also where you can set waypoints and stuff like that. Do takeoff checklist on the roll to the runway. Set up HSI for takeoff. TACAN boxed, Plan boxed, 20 for scale, put tacan offset in if you know you’ll need it for a point like Waade for example. Etc.
SET UP IFF if you haven’t already. There is a pdf in box that shows how to set it up.
Takeoff Checklist: (done on way to runway) All these bold checklist steps are said out loud over ICS..
1. Control Aug - All, light extinguished
2. Anti-Skid Switch - On, Light On
3. Flaps/Slats - Set Half, Indicating Half, Slats Visually Down
4. Trim - Zero, Zero, 2-3 deg Nose Up
5. Canopy - Closed, Locked, Light Out, Seal Inflated
6. Harness:
                “Attached 8 Points, Pins Removed, Ready to Arm in Front”
                R/C: “Attached 8 Points, Pins Removed, Armed in Back”
                “Armed in Front, Takeoff Checks Complete”
Contact Base (Ch1) on Aux and Ground (Ch 2) on Pri prior to leaving the Marshall Area: ATC or Base Responses in Blue
Ch 1 Aux: “Base, 01 Taxi” You just say the last two numbers
“201 have a good flight”
Ch 2 Pri: “Kingsville Ground, Blazer 201, taxi with alpha”
“Blazer 201 taxi to 13R via Bravo Alpha”
“Taxi to 13R via Bravo Alpha, Blazer 201”
1500 ft prior to hold short (Abeam the arresting gear), auto switch to tower on channel 3 on PRI.
Position yourself at the hold short on the far left of the taxiway with your nose pointed at the windsock, (for runway 13R), your nose will be pointed slightly toward the center of the taxiway (Maybe like a 45 deg cut or slightly less). This will offset you so that the exhaust from the tailpipe is not directed at any a/c that may pull up behind you. This also allows them to go around you on the right side of the taxiway should you be held up at the hold short for any reason. You only have to do this if you are told to hold short. Call tower for takeoff before even turning into the hold short area so that maybe you can just roll right from taxi out onto the runway. If you do have to hold short at a 45 deg angle, use the concrete crack closest to the edge of the taxiway to line your nosewheel up with and slowly move all the way up to the hold short and then use a full boot of hi-gain NWS and inside differential braking to stay close to the edge and get that 45 deg cut. Then set parking brake. You may feel the jet start to shake in the front. This is the nosewheel shaking back in forth because it is not straight. To fix this just release the parking brake and let off the brakes to allow the jet to roll a tiny bit forward and then hit the brakes and reset the parking brake and it will be gone.
