## PRE START CHECKLIST
1. BATT 1 & BATT 2....ON — ALTERNATE EACH BATTERY TO CHECK INDIVIDUAL VOLTAGE; 24-29VDC
2. MASTER ALERT LT....OUT
3. BOTH BATT SWITCHES....ON — • INSERT USB INTO USB SOCKET • UPLOAD/USB UPLOAD/SELECT AT WYPT • WHEN UPLOAD COMPLETED, STOW USB
4. SEAT [PEDALS]....ADJUST
5. FUEL QUANTITY....CHECK
6. ADVISORY LIGHTS....SKID LT ON
7. FIRE LIGHT....OUT
8. ICS....CHECK — F/C: "ICS CHECK, SWITCHES SET, NORM, (FUEL)" R/C: "LOUD & CLEAR, SWITCHES SET, NORM, BOTH, (FUEL)"
9. WARNING/CAUTION LIGHTS (ENSURE THE FOLLOWING ON): HYD, CANOPY, OXYGEN, OIL PRES, GENERATOR, HYD FAIL, LP PUMP, F PRES, SKID (highlighted on the light panel graphic)
F/C: "LIGHTS AND TONES"
10. LTS/TONES/AOA INDXERS....TEST
11. HYD1/HYD2/BRAKE PRESS....0/0/1250
12. FLAP POS LT....MATCHES FLAPS
13. GEAR POS LTS....ON
14. COMM/NAV TRANS....AS DESIRED
15. AOA....NO OFF FLAG
16. POS/FORM LIGHTS....AS DESIRED
17. PINKY SWITCH....AS REQ'D
18. HELMET VISOR....DOWN
19. CANOPY....AS DESIRED
20. PADDLE SWITCH....PRESS/RELEASE
21. NWS CAUTION LIGHT....CHECK ON
22. VERIFY THROTTLE OFF
## ENGINE START CHECKLIST
F/C: "THROTTLE OFF, GTS" R/C "CLEAR"
1. GTS BUTTON....PRESS MOMENTARILY • GTS LIGHT IN 20 SEC
2. ENGINE SWITCH....START • READY LIGHT IN 15 SEC
3. THROTTLE....IDLE @ 17-20% N2 • MONITOR ENG INST/OIL PRES LT OUT
4. VOLTMETER....✓27-29V
5. OBOGS/ANTI-G SWITCH....ON
6. HUD/MFDS....ON/SET BRIGHTNESS
7. UHF/VHF RADIOS....ON
8. VOR/ILS/TACAN....ON/AS DESIRED
9. IFF....STBY
## POST START CHECKLIST
1. THROTTLE....ADVANCE SLOWLY TO 70%
2. HYD2 RESET BUTTON....PRESS
3. HYD PRESSURES....3000 PSI/LT OUT
4. FUEL CTRL SWITCH....HOLD IN TEST • M FUEL LIGHT ON/RPM DROP (6% MAX)
5. FUEL CTRL SWITCH....NORM • (LT OUT, PREVIOUS RPM)
6. THROTTLE....IDLE • EGT DROP ~50°C/~3%RPM RISE/RPM±2% OF IDLE (BLEED VALVE CLOSED)
7. AIRCRAFT EXCEEDANCES....CHECK • MENU/BIT/MAINT....SELECT • VERIFY....."NO EXCEEDENCE/OVERFLOW"
8. ALIGNMENT PROGRESS....CHECK • MENU/DATA/ACFT....SELECT • VERIFY..."4 SATS/QUAL COUNTING DN"
 - IF ALN QUALITY NOT DECREMENTING • VERIFY A/C PRESENT POS....WYPT 0 • WYP ZERO....ENTER • RST OPTION....SELECT • AFTER 50 SEC..VERIFY QUAL DECREMENTING
9. IF QUAL DECREMENTING AND 4 SATS CTRL AUG BIT....PERFORM • PADDLE SW....PRESS/CAUG LT ON • CONT AUG SW....RESET/C AUG OUT • CONTR AUG SW....SBI TO ALL • CAUG LT ON, OUT IN 120SEC MAX
10. STAB TRIM....CHECK • PITCH TRIM....-3 TO +8° • STBY STAB TRIM....CHECK • PITCH TRIM....2-3° NOSE UP
11. AILERON TRIM....CHECK/NEUTRAL
12. WHEN C AUG IBIT FINISHED/LT OUT • RUD TRIM....CHECK/SET NEUTRAL
13. STBY ATT INDICATOR....ERECT
14. LAW....BIT CHECK/SET — SNA: "GOOD TONE, GOOD BIT"
15. BINGO....SET
16. STBY ALT....SET BARO PRESS • CHECK W/IN 75' OF FLD ELEV
17. HUD ALT....✓ W/IN 10' FLD ELEV • IF NOT W/IN 10', ADJUST BARO DLTA PER PCL
18. BIT DISPLAY....NOTE DEGDS
19. WAYPOINTS....PROGRAM
20. NAV SOURCE....SELECT
21. OBOGS ELECTRONIC BIT....PERFORM • PRESS 1 TO 2 SEC/OXYGEN LT ON • LIGHT OUT W/IN 15 SEC
22. OBOGS INTEGRITY GROUND CHECK: • O2 MASK(S)....ON • OBOGS FLOW SELECTOR(S)....ON • ADR EVENT RECORD BUTTON....PRESS — IF O2 LT ILLUMINATES W/IN 60 SEC PERFORM OXYGEN WARNING LIGHT EP
23. ANTI-G TEST....PRESS
## PLANE CAPTAIN CHECKLIST
F/C: "WATCH YOUR KNEES" R/C: "CLEAR"
1. FLIGHT CONTROLS....CHECK (WIPE OUT COCKPIT signal)
 - WHEN PLANE CAPTAIN SIGNALS:
2. NWS....ENGAGE
3. SPEEDBRAKES....EXTEND • FLAPS/SLAT LEVER....FULL • HOOK....DOWN • LBAR....AS REQ'D
4. CHECK: • FLAP INDICATOR....FULL • SLATS....DOWN • SP BRK / HOOK / FULL LIGHTS....ON
5. SPEEDBRAKES....RETRACT • FLAPS/SLATS LEVER....HALF • HOOK....UP • LBAR....AS REQ'D (HOOK UP signal)
6. CHECK: • FLAPS....HALF • SLATS....DOWN • SP BRK / FULL LIGHTS....OUT • HOOK LIGHT....OUT IN 6 SEC
 - WHEN PLANE CAPTAIN SIGNALS: F/C: "CANOPY" R/C: "CLEAR"
7. CANOPY....CLOSE
## TAXI/INSTRUMENT CHECKLIST
1. PARKING BRAKE....RELEASE (BRAKES OFF signal)
2. WHEEL BRAKES....CHECK
3. NWS....CHECK
4. FLIGHT INSTRUMENTS....CHECK/SET • AOA....CHECK • STBY MACH/AIRSPEED....ZERO • STBY VSI....ZERO/ERR ±100 • STBY ALTIMETER....SET/ERR ±75 • STBY ATTITUDE INDICATOR....ERECT • STBY NEEDLE/BALL....MATCHES MFD • HUD/MFD HDG/STBY HDGS....CHECK • MFD....CHECK/SET • HUD/MFD ALTIMETER....SET/ERR ±10 • COMM/NAV EQUIP....CHECK/SET • CLOCK....SET/RUNNING
"ALT SET, VOR SET, TCN SET, IFF SET" "TAXI & INSTRUMENT CHECKS COMPLETE"
## TAKEOFF CHECKLIST
1. CONTROL AUG....ALL
2. ANTI-SKID SWITCH....ON/LIGHT ON
3. FLAPS/SLATS....SET ½/ INDICATING ½/SLATS VISUALLY DOWN
4. TRIM....0/0/2-3° NOSE UP
5. CANOPY....CLOSED/LOCKED/LT OUT
6. HARNESS: F/C: "ATTACHED 8 POINTS, PINS REMOVED, READY TO ARM IN FRONT" R/C: "ATTACHED 8 POINTS, PINS REMOVED, ARMED IN BACK" F/C: "ARMED IN FRONT" "TAKEOFF CHECKS COMPLETE"
## AFTER LANDING CHECKS (WHEN CLEAR OF RUNWAY)
1. SEATS....SAFE — "READY TO SAFE IN FRONT" "SAFE IN BACK" "SAFE IN FRONT"
2. SPEEDBRAKES....RETRACT
3. FLAPS/SLATS....UP
4. TRIM....SET TO 0
5. OBOGS FLOW SELECTOR....OFF
6. NAV/IFF EQUIP....OFF • HUD....OFF • PITOT HEAT....OFF • STROBES....OFF • TAXI LIGHT....AS REQ'D • VCR SWITCH....OFF
## BEFORE ENGINE SHUTDOWN
1. BIT PAGE....CHECK
2. PERFORM IBIT ON DEGD EQUIP....RECORD
3. LAW....OFF
4. MAINT PAGE....CHECK
5. AIRCRAFT EXCEEDENCE....CHECK — VERIFY "NO EXCEEDENCE/OVERFLOW"
6. RIGHT MFD....OFF
7. UHF/VHF RADIOS....OFF
8. VCR SWITCH....OFF
9. STBY ATT GYRO....CAGED (OFF FLAG)
10. IDLE RPM....± 2% OF NATOPS
## ENGINE SHUTDOWN
1. PARKING BRAKE....SET
2. OBOGS/ANTI-G SWITCH....OFF
3. THROTTLE....OFF (CUT ENGINE signal)
4. MONITOR EGT/RPM/FF FOR NORMAL SHUTDOWN
5. CANOPY....AS DESIRED
 - 30 SECONDS AFTER PRK BRK SET:
6. GINA POWER....OFF
7. LEFT MFD....OFF
 - 45 SECONDS AFTER THROTTLE OFF:
8. ENGINE SWITCH (F/C ONLY)....OFF
9. FUEL SHUTOFF HANDLE....PULL
10. POS/FORM LIGHTS....OFF
11. CHECK BRK PRESS ACCUM....MIN 2700PSI
 - 50 SECONDS AFTER THROTTLE OFF:
12. CTRL STICK...1/2 Lat Defection at 1 cycle per 2 secs. Neutalize when HYD1 reaches 0 PSI (WIPE OUT COCKPIT signal)
13. PINKY SWITCH....OFF
14. CONSOLE/MIP LITS....OFF
15. MDL....REMOVE  VCR TAPE....REMOVE
17. USB/DOWNLOAD....INSERT/INITIATE  USB DEVICE....UNPLUG/STOW  CONTROL PANEL....SECURE COVER
20. BATT SWITCHES (2&2)....OFF
21. SEAT/MDC....PINNED
