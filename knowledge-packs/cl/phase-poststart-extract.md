# 2. Post-Start, Clearance & Plane Captain

## Doc
Post-Start Checklist:
1. Give 70% check signal to lineman (sideways 2/peace sign)
2. Throttle slowly to 70%, at the same time quickly do the following:
   1. RMFD: Menu-BIT-Maintenance
   2. LMFD: Data-Aircraft
3. When RPM gets to 70%: Hyd2-Reset
Look for hyd2 pressure to go to 3000 psi and hyd caution light to go out
4. Fuel Control Switch - Hold in test
                Check RPM (max drop of 6%). RPM may momentarily increase. Make sure M Fuel light comes on while holding in test. Now go back to norm and observe that RPM returns to 70% and M Fuel light goes out.
5. Throttle - Idle
                Check N2 56.5+/-2% and EGT drops 50 deg C (this means the bleed valve closed on the run up to 70%)
6. Check MFD’s for Exceedances/Overflows and Alignment
                RMFD should show No exceedances (A/C EXCEEDANCE (blank)) and No overflows (ADR MEMORY OVERFLOW (blank)). LMFD should show 4 sats (GPS SAT 4) and Quals counting down. Quals should be decrementing down to 00.59. C AUG BIT will not work in sim until the quals are below 3.00. This is not an issue in the jet. Sometimes it take a second for quals to start counting down. If that is the case I will just say quals not counting down yet or something along those lines.
                “No Exceedances, No Overflows, 4 SATS, QUALS Counting Down”
7. Control Aug BIT
   1. Paddle Switch - Press
                        C Aug Caution illuminates (NWS Caution should still be on)
   2. Contr Aug Switch - Reset (Release from reset and let the switch sit in SBI for a second then go to all. The C aug caution will be out after the reset and then the C Aug caution illuminates when you go to all and the BIT is started).
                        While doing this, look at rudder trim needle and make sure it jumps from the 6 o’clock position to 12 o’clock and C Aug Caution Illuminates. That means the BIT is running now for 2 minutes (BIT is done when C Aug caution extinguishes). You now have 2 minutes to finish the rest before plane captain checks. Once the BIT is done you need to move on to plane captain checks and do the rest later.
8. OBOGS Electronic BIT (Just simulate in Sim don’t actually do it)
                Bring hand way back left, press button on back left wall for 1 to 2 seconds. Oxygen warning light comes on, punch master alert out, oxygen light should go away within 15 seconds. (Don’t wait move on)
9. Anti-G Test Button - Press
10.  Trim Checks (Minus rudder trim because you cannot do that when C AUG BIT is running)
   1. Pitch trim - go to full nose down trim. Then go to full nose up trim.
   2. STBY Stab trim/Aileron Trim - lift guard cover and make sure doing that stops your pitch trim adjustment. Close cover. Do this on the way to nose down trim and then again on the way to nose up trim. Then check the standby by moving the switch momentarily in both directions.
   3. Aileron Trim - Check both directions, set neutral, you can do this at the same time as you are checking the standby pitch trim.
   4. Pitch trim - Set for T/O (2-3 deg nose up), most instructors like 2 deg nose up in the jet.
11. Oxygen Mask - On
12.  OBOGS Flow Selector - On
                Put visor down. “Mask on, Flow on” You need to have the mask on for about a minute. Usually when I start the PC checks and do the wipe out and all that once they direct me to raise my hands is when I’ll reach down and turn the flow off and then take the mask off for the rest of the time until we are getting close to the hold short.
13.  ECS Air Flow Check
                Turn air flow knob off and verify no flow. Turn airflow knob back on and verify flow.
14.  Ch 29 Pri: “Kingsville Clearance, Blazer 201, Premont 2 on request ready to copy”
                Write down clearance and read it back. Go to normal on IFF, press mode and turn mode 3 and c off and then go to home and punch in squawk code. Punch altitude clearance in command altitude on ADI. Once done with clearance go right to channel 2 (ground). You can also hold ELS MS at this time and check to make sure flight ID is set to the correct squadron. If not change it because this is what shows up on ADSB. There is a powerpoint on box that goes into more detail on these IFF functions.
15.  LMFD: ADI (put altitude you are cleared to in C ALT)
16.  RMFD: Menu-BIT
                Check no degradations. Box RADALT to test the LAW. You should hear an aural tone. “Good tone, Good BIT”. Sometimes I just hit this on the taxi to final checks because RADALT hasn’t shown up yet on this screen but regardless don’t change the right MFD to anything other than the BIT page until you have done this test so that you remember to do it.
17.  RMFD: HSI (Box sequence. In the sim sequence will already be boxed and your working areas will show up. In the jet you have to box sequence every time.). Go to 20 scale for departure and box TCN (WPT on future flights).
18.  Once C Aug Caution extinguishes: Rudder Trim - check both directions, set neutral (C aug checks the rudder trim so you are really just checking the switch. A lot of people don’t really check this anymore.
If C aug caution extinguishes before you have done all of this then stop what you are doing and move on to PC checks and do the rest in marshal.
Plane Captain Checklist: Initiate when C AUG caution extinguishes
1. “Watch your knees” R/C: “Clear”
2. Signal lineman for Plane Captain checks with a thumbs up
3. PC Signals for Wipe Out (Looks like stirring a bowl)
                Two hands on stick. Bring full left, full forward while holding full left, full right while holding full forward, to the center while holding full forward, straight back to full aft, full right while holding full aft, full left while holding full aft, to the center while holding full aft, and then all the way around in a circle CCW.
4. PC Signals for Rudder Pedals (Pushing hands/arms forward and alternating)
                Check that NWS Caution is illuminated (meaning NWS is off). Check for full deflection of rudder pedals/no binding.
5. PC Signals for NWS and three down (touches nose and does a bunch of other random quick hand signals)
                Press NWS button (NWS caution goes out). Hook-Down, Flaps-Full, Speed Brake-Full. Verify all positions with the appropriate indicator lights (Red hook light on (this is because the hook hits the ground and isn’t as far down as it would be in the air), flaps full light, speed brake full lights).
6. Hands-Up
                Hands must be above rail when PC does walk around. Use this time to set LAW (200), CRS (130), HDG (Set as lead radial for SID) and Bingo (1.8 for joker as a heads up for a bingo of 1.6 for most BI and TR flights. 1.6 is when you need to head out of area by to have fuel for approaches or pattern work). Start practicing good LAW and BINGO discipline. LAW should be set to the next thing before it goes off (except for 5000 foot platform you let that one go off) and BINGO should also be set before it goes off (1.8, 1.6, .5).
7. Connect Upper Fittings
8. Do Flight Instrument Checks below if you have time, minus the comparison to the STBY heading since the canopy must be closed to see that
9. Program Waypoints (As required) (Can also do this during taxi when comfortable and also in final checks or marshal)(Disclaimer: Early on in the syllabus you might get yelled at if you try to do stuff during PC checks and final checks. The only things that are technically approved to do during PC checks when you are supposed to have your hands up is manipulation of the data entry panel (law, crs, bingo, etc) and attaching your upper garters. I do a lot more than this such as waypoints sometimes on the MFD and finishing up lower garters and lower koch fittings for strap in. If you decide to do this you just have to be very careful not to bump the stick or manipulate anything that would change a configuration on the jet.
                RMFD: Data-GPS
                Set waypoint 1 to active duty waypoint, waypoint 2 to RICTO, waypoint 3 maybe to Waade if you anticipate needing it, etc.
10. Do a Sweep with Hands still up if PC is still walking around
                OBOGS/Anti-G, Flows (if mask is off is flow selector off? - yes it should be), Looking at left panel: Contr Aug is in all, flaps are down, anti-skid is on, lights are working, speed brake is out, up on 2 on pri, right side switches look good, i’m strapped in. This is all just a sanity check if you have the time. See discussion above about potentially doing waypoints and finishing strap in during PC checks/taxi/final checkers so that you eventually can work up to the point that you don’t need to stop in marshal and can just roll through it (they do not expect you to be even close to this fast in TR’s so prioritize doing things correctly and safely. Please don’t rush and bump something you aren’t supposed to during PC checks or final checks or try to do too much during the taxi that you are distracted. The safest bet is to do nothing extra during PC checks/taxi/final checks especially earlier on. There will be a lot going on. This is also the expectation so no need to feel rushed). Also no need to do anything like the lower garters during the start early on like discussed above. You can taxi the jet out from the apes without being strapped in and you can finish all of this stuff in marshal. That is what marshal is for.
11. Plane Captain Shows 4 Tools - Give them a thumbs up
                PC shows you 4 tools so you can verify they have them all and there isn’t a FOD hazard. PC will tell you to bring everything up. Hook-Up, Flaps-Half, Speed Brake-Retracted. Verify all positions with the appropriate indicator lights (hook light out in 6 seconds, flap light at half, speed brake light out)
12. PC Shows tools again - Give them a thumbs up
                Signal for closing canopy: Tap the top of your head. Lineman will give same signal back to you. “Canopy” R/C: “Rail Clear”. Close canopy and lock it. Ensure canopy caution light extinguishes.
