## Engine Limitation Notes

### Card 19
**Q:** 1. Bleed valve ______________. Nominal RPM will vary depending on engine loading, air bleed, ambient conditions, and altitude. With airflow knob in the OFF position, idle RPM will operate approximately ______% higher than normal.
**A:** 1. Bleed valve closed. Nominal RPM will vary depending on engine loading, air bleed, ambient conditions, and altitude. With airflow knob in the OFF position, idle RPM will operate approximately 2% higher than normal.

### Card 20
**Q:** 2. Maximum overshoot of _______ °C for _______ seconds.
**A:** 2. Maximum overshoot of 20 °C for 10 seconds.

### Card 21
**Q:** 3. Maximum overshoot of _______ °C for _______ seconds (NOTE: The EGT/RPM warning light will operate at _______ ± _______ °C).
**A:** 3. Maximum overshoot of 50 °C for 10 seconds (NOTE: The EGT/RPM warning light will operate at 650 ± 8 °C).

### Card 22
**Q:** 4. Maximum shift in engine idle RPM between the Poststart and Engine Shutdown checks, corrected for standard day, is ______%. Idle RPM will normally increase approximately ______% for every _________ foot increase in altitude and ______% for every _______ °C in ambient temperature.
**A:** 4. Maximum shift in engine idle RPM between the Poststart and Engine Shutdown checks, corrected for standard day, is 2%. Idle RPM will normally increase approximately 1% for every 1,500 foot increase in altitude and 1% for every 10 °C in ambient temperature.

### Card 23
**Q:** 5. Transient/acceleration limits apply to any acceleration whether from idle to MRT or any increment in between. EGT may rise to _______ °C during or immediately following the acceleration, but must decrease to _______ °C or less within _______ seconds after the engine reaches its maximum RPM.
**A:** 5. Transient/acceleration limits apply to any acceleration whether from idle to MRT or any increment in between. EGT may rise to 645 °C during or immediately following the acceleration, but must decrease to 610 °C or less within 20 seconds after the engine reaches its maximum RPM.

**A:** 1a. Throttle shall be at idle for: Abrupt (0.5 seconds or less from neutral to full back stick) pulls to full back stick.

### Card 29

**A:** 1b. Throttle shall be at idle for: Abrupt full lateral stick inputs at full back stick.

### Card 30
**Q:** 1c. Throttle shall be at idle for: Airspeeds less than _______ KIAS at altitudes above _____________ feet MSL.
**A:** 1c. Throttle shall be at idle for: Airspeeds less than 85 KIAS at altitudes above 15,000 feet MSL.

### Card 31
**Q:** 2. For airpseeds ____ to ____ KIAS at altitudes above _______ feet MSL; ______ throttle movements.
**A:** 2. For airspeeds 85 to 150 KIAS at altitudes above 35,000 feet MSL; slow throttle movements.

### Card 32

**A:** 3. Except in an emergency, engine shall be stabilized at idle for at least 30 seconds prior to shutdown.

## Starting Limitations

### Card 37
**Q:** 1. Engine start is prohibited with the throttle _____________ the ground idle detent.
**A:** 1. Engine start is prohibited with the throttle above the ground idle detent.

### Card 38
**Q:** 2. Minimum N₂ RPM required for engine start is _______%.
**A:** 2. Minimum N₂ RPM required for engine start is 15%.

### Card 39
**Q:** 3. Engine light-off during a normal start must occur within _______ seconds after moving the throttle to _________.
**A:** 3. Engine light-off during a normal start must occur within 15 seconds after moving the throttle to idle.

### Card 40
**Q:** 4. For starting, minimum voltage for each battery is ________ volts.
**A:** 4. For starting, minimum voltage for each battery is 24 volts.

### Card 41
**Q:** 5. No more than _________ successive engine start attempts may be performed. Following the _________ start attempt, a _______ minute cool down period must be observed before attempting another engine start.
**A:** 5. No more than three successive engine start attempts may be performed. Following the third start attempt, a 30 minute cool down period must be observed before attempting another engine start.

## GTS Limitations

### Card 44
**Q:** 1. GTS start with ________________ _______________ is prohibited.
**A:** 1. GTS start with external power is prohibited.

### Card 45
**Q:** 2. Minimum interval between each GTS start attempt is _____-_______________. After ________ consecutive start attempts, a minimum interval of ______-______________ is required before any further start attempts.
**A:** 2. Minimum interval between each GTS start attempt is 3 minutes. After three consecutive start attempts, a minimum interval of 30 minutes is required before any further start attempts.

### Card 46
**Q:** 3. Additional GTS start attempts after an unsuccessful GTS start attempt are prohibited until the GTS ____________ ____________ and _______________ _______________ are inspected and any residual fuel is removed, and the __________ ____________ are inspected for discoloration and warpage.
**A:** 3. Additional GTS start attempts after an unsuccessful GTS start attempt are prohibited until the GTS exhaust duct and compressor section are inspected and any residual fuel is removed, and the RAT doors are inspected for discoloration and warpage.
