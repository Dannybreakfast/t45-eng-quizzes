How to brief an approach plate

1. Before you open the plate

  • Currency: check the d-TPP cycle dates on the plate margin (effective/expiration) and the amendment number/date bottom-left. Check NOTAMs.
  • Weather: ATIS (KNQI 282.25) — runway in use, ceiling/visibility, altimeter, winds. Decide which approach is likely and whether you can legally start it (OPNAV 3710.7 weather/alternate rules for your instrument rating).
  • Category: approach category = 1.3 Vso / Vref at max landing weight (AIM 5-4-7): A <91, B 91–120, C 121–140, D 141–165, E >165 KIAS. A T-45C on-speed (~17 units AOA) final is roughly 120–135 KIAS, so brief Cat C. If you'll fly faster (e.g. circling at higher speed), use the higher category — never a lower one. HI- plates only publish C/D/E.

2. Top-down briefing order (the "strip, plan, profile, mins" flow)

  1. Title & airport: procedure name (e.g. "ILS Z RWY 13R" — the letter distinguishes procedures to the same runway), airport, date/amendment.
  2. Briefing strip: primary NAVAID frequency/channel and ident (LOC 110.9 I-NQI Chan 46; TACAN NQI Chan 125; NOG Chan 63 on TACAN Y 17R!), final approach course, runway length, TDZE, airport elevation.
  3. Notes: "TACAN required", ALS-inop vis increases, cautions (short intermediate segments), the T (nonstandard takeoff mins/obstacle notes) symbol.
  4. Missed approach (briefed early so it's in your head): the first action verbatim — e.g. "Climb to 500, then climbing right turn to 3000, NQI R-184 to BRAMA and hold."
  5. Comms: ATIS, approach, tower, ground, clearance; ASR/PAR availability.
  6. Plan view: MSA (emergency only, 1000 ft clearance within 25 NM), ESA, IAF(s) and feeders, holding/hold-in-lieu of PT (altitudes, max speed), arcs and lead radials, IF, FAF, terrain/obstacles.
  7. Profile: altitudes at each fix (underlined = minimum, overbar = maximum), glideslope intercept (lightning bolt = precision FAF) vs Maltese cross (non-precision FAF), stepdowns, VDP (V), descent angle/TCH, distances, MAP.
  8. Minimums: your category's DA or MDA, visibility, HAT/HAA. HAT = DA/MDA − TDZE; HAA = circling MDA − airport elevation. Note any ✱ footnotes.
  9. Airport sketch: runway layout, lighting (ALSF-1 on 35R; HIRL/REIL all; TDZL/CL 17L), elevation, then taxi plan.

3. Flying it — rules to remember

  • Procedure turn / HILPT: not flown when vectored to final, on a NoPT route, or cleared straight-in (FAR 91.175(j), AIM 5-4-9).
  • Below DA/MDA: only when continuously in position to land with normal maneuvers, flight visibility ≥ published, and required visual references in sight (FAR 91.175(c)). Otherwise go missed at DA / at the MAP.
  • VDP: don't leave MDA before the VDP unless a normal descent is possible (AIM 5-4-5).
  • Circling: reversed-C = expanded circling protected area (Cat C ≈ 2.7 NM at ≤1000 MSL). KNQI radar circling only from PAR W/O GS and ASR; circling to 31L NA at night.
  • Missed approach from a circle: initial climbing turn toward the landing runway, then the published missed (AIM 5-4-21).

4. Departures

  • Brief the KINGSVILLE FOUR: runway heading (130/175/310/355), the NQI 7 DME arc direction, the transition radial (CRP R-038 cross JERIR ≥8000; HOBOZ R-339; JUBAK R-125 / RICTO R-225 are local SUA-entry only), DEP CON 121.05/266.8.
  • No climb gradient is published on the SID; check the takeoff minimums page for obstacle notes (KNQI lists close-in obstacles only). Standard is 200 ft/NM.
  • Lost comms: per the SID — if no transmissions one minute after departure, climb to assigned altitude, proceed via the 7 DME arc / departure radial to the fix and transition; climb to filed altitude once established on the departure radial (FAR 91.185 logic).

Training aid; verify against NATOPS, CNATRA instrument FTI and the current plate.
