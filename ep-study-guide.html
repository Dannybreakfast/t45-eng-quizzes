EP Study Guide — T-45C Emergency Procedures

How to use this guide: every EP below starts with the current IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) immediate-action wording. That is the answer to memorize. After it come the gouge notes (source page tags such as p.65) and the Quizlet facts (EP1 = set 783093107, EP2 = set 783149178).

Source age. The student gouge PDF dates from about 2007 (old 06X/11X EP exams), and both Quizlet sets copy that gouge. Where they disagree with IC 21/43, this guide marks them OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS. Memorize the IC 21/43 wording. For anything outside the 22 immediate-action items, defer to your current NATOPS/PCL.

2. Ground: Emergency Shutdown/Egress, FIRE / GTS FIRE / TP HOT on deck

Emergency Shutdown / Egress

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN/EGRESS — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - Off
2. Engine switch - Off
3. Fuel Shutoff Handle – Pull
4. Ejection Seats – Safe
5. Batt switches - Off

Gouge notes - Use it for a FIRE light, GTS FIRE, or TP HOT on deck. All three = execute emergency shutdown (p.58 handwritten, p.59 #7, p.64 #13). - "What requires an immediate shutdown on deck w/out 2ndary indications — FIRE light illums" (p.5 #11, EP1 #11). - FIRE light on the ground: "get out of airplane and don't worry about telling anyone over the radio… no comms in procedure" (p.60 #25). - TP HOT is a caution (yellow), not a warning (red). Don't get drawn in by "which warning light causes you to egress" (p.60 #26). - Engine fire indications on startup: FIRE light, increased FF, rapidly increasing EGT → all of the above (p.59 #13).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.6 / EP1 #22: "(A,E,B,D,F, if different use PCL!) 1) Throttle OFF, 2) Engine Switch-OFF (both Cockpits), 3) Fuel Shutoff – PULL, 4) BATT switches – OFF, 5) Emergency Egress". It has no Ejection Seats – Safe step. The p.77 EP 11X on-deck fire / TP HOT version reads "a. Throttle – Off b. Fuel Shutoff Handle – Pull c. Battery Switches – Off d. Egress", with no engine switch except a handwritten "ENG Switch OFF".

GTS Fire Warning Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — GTS FIRE WARNING LIGHT — MEMORIZE
GROUND
1. Execute emergency shutdown / egress
IN-FLIGHT
1. Engine switch - off
  • The GTS FIRE light means an overtemperature in the GTS compartment (>300°C outside the GTS container), not a fire in the burner can (p.62 E, p.54 #124).
  • In-flight: "Engine Switch Off is the first step" (p.59 #12, p.65).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.76 #30 GTS Fire on deck: "a. Engine switch – Off b. Throttle – Off c. Fuel Shutoff Handle – Pull d. Battery Switches – Off e. Egress". Current ground answer: execute emergency shutdown/egress (Throttle first, and includes Ejection Seats – Safe).

TP Hot Caution Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — TP HOT CAUTION LIGHT — MEMORIZE
GROUND
1. Execute emergency shutdown / egress
IN-FLIGHT
1. Throttle – minimum for safe flight
  • TP HOT = temperature outside the tail cone, inside the fuselage, above 150°C (p.54 #125, p.37). There is no verification of an internal tailpipe overtemp (p.3).
  • "When flying @ 15K, TP HOT light – Throttle idle (min safe flight)" (p.5 #12, EP1 #12). Consistent with the current wording.

3. Starts: Clear Engine / Abnormal Start

Clear Engine Procedure / Abnormal Start / TP Fire on Shutdown

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — CLEAR ENGINE PROCEDURE/ABNORMAL START/TP FIRE ON SHUTDOWN — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - off
  • Overspeed on start → Clear Engine: Throttle Off is the first step, not Throttle Idle (p.59 #11, p.65).
  • Wet start: no light-off within 15 s of throttle idle (p.5 #1, p.62 G, EP1 #1).
  • Hung start: RPM stagnates around 45% while EGT keeps rising (p.4, p.56).
  • Hot start: FF/RPM normal but EGT rapidly to 550 or to 570 for >10 s (p.56 #168).
  • Start limits: FF 300–400 · EGT 550 (570 for 10 s) · RPM 52 ± 2 within 30 s of idle (p.56). GTS light within 20 s, READY light within 15 s, light-off within 15 s (p.36, p.37).

4. Takeoff: Abort, fire on takeoff

Abort

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — ABORT — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - idle
2. Speed brakes - extend
3. Brakes - apply
4. Hook – down (if required)
5. Brakes – release prior to crossing the arresting gear
  • For an aborted T/O with no blown tire: do not turn anti-skid off (p.62 C). With a blown tire: anti-skid off (EP1 #6 "set ANTI-SKID ON – False").
  • Abort for red lights on takeoff when below refusal speed (p.60 #28). Max abort speed = can commence a stop on the remaining runway without using the overrun or arresting gear (p.2 #33).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.5 #9 / EP1 #9: "IDLE, BOARDS retract, BRAKES, HOOK (1000 ft. prior)". Current: Speed brakes – extend, and finish with Brakes – release prior to crossing the arresting gear. EP2 #25 ("Idle, extend, brakes as req, hook 1000 ft prior") gets extend right.

Fire Warning Light: Takeoff

See the full IC 21/43 FIRE WARNING LIGHT procedure in §8. On takeoff: decided to stop → Abort; fire confirmed and unable to abort → Eject. - The first indication of a fire on takeoff is the FIRE light (TRUE) (p.3, p.47). - Gouge: "Fire on takeoff = Abort, if unable and the fire is confirmed then EJECT" (p.60 #19, p.66 #21, p.74 #11).

5. Catapult / carrier

Emergency Catapult Flyaway

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — EMERGENCY CATAPULT FLYAWAY — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - MRT
2. Maintain 24 units AOA
If engine failed, or unable to stop settle:
3. Eject
  • Gouge wording: "Throttle full, check 24 AOA, if settle continues Eject" (p.5 #13, EP1 #13); "throttle check full forward, 24 AOA" (EP2 #28). Memorize "Throttle – MRT."
  • Engine failure on the cat stroke: Eject. If unable to eject: ditch straight ahead (p.5 #15, EP1 #15, EP2 #13).
  • Ditching configuration: gear up, flaps full down (p.59 #8, p.65).
  • Launch bar warning light after cat launch: divert to shore and make a minimum-descent-rate (flared) landing with the arresting wires removed (EP2 #29, p.75 #20). With the L-BAR extended, land past the arresting gear (p.72).
  • Launch bar actuation: electrically controlled, hydraulically (HYD 1) extended, mechanical spring retracted (p.3, p.54 #140, handwritten correction p.60). EP1 #14 / p.5 #14 says the reverse ("mechanically extended, hydraulically retracted"), so the gouge contradicts itself.

6. Engine failure and airstart

Engine Failure

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — ENGINE FAILURE — MEMORIZE
If below 1,500 feet AGL and airspeed below 180KIAS:
1. Eject
Otherwise:
2. Execute airstart
  • "Just flamed out, airspeed is 150 and at 1000' you should – Eject" (EP1 #8). "Have enough time for Airstart need 1500' AGL and 180 KIAS" (p.38).
  • Immediate airstart on any engine failure, except below 1,500 ft / 180 KIAS (p.65).
  • Engine seizure: RPM rapidly decays to zero (p.54 #119).

Airstart

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — AIRSTART — MEMORIZE
1. Emergency oxygen green ring(s) - pull
2. Throttle - off
Simultaneously perform steps *3. and *4.
3. GTS Start Button – Press
4. Throttle - Idle
If unsuccessful (no relight within 30 seconds after moving throttle to idle or stabilized EGT exceeds limits):
5. Throttle – off (allow 30 seconds to drain if practical)
If above 13% RPM and 250 KIAS, repeat steps *3 and *4.
Otherwise, proceed to step 6.

Gouge airstart envelopes (p.58, p.67, p.73, p.76):

Type Envelope
Immediate Any altitude
Windmill Below 25,000 ft MSL, 13% N2 minimum, 250 KIAS or greater
Assisted (GTS) Below 15,000 ft MSL, < 20% RPM, GTS light on
  • Airstart EGT limit 600°C (650°C max for 10 s overshoot). Ignition stays on 30 s after GTS release. GTS light within 28 s in flight (p.37, p.39).
  • Stall vs flameout: for a stall you do not perform an airstart and do not pull emergency O2. For a flameout you do those first (p.65).
  • No airstart for a fire in flight (p.61, p.65).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.77 #33 "Immediate Airstart: a. Throttle – Off / Simultaneously perform steps 2 and 3 / b. GTS – Press and Hold / c. Throttle – Idle". It has no emergency-oxygen step, says "Press and Hold", and has no "if unsuccessful" branch. EP2 #14 ("Pull Emerg. Oxygen actuator, throttle off, GTS button, idle") is closer to the current procedure.

7. Compressor stall / EGT-RPM warning light

Compressor Stall or EGT/RPM Warning Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — COMPRESSOR STALL OR EGT/RPM WARNING LIGHT — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - idle
2. EGT/RPM - check
If EGT is greater than 450°C for more than 6 seconds at idle:
3. Execute engine failure procedure
If EGT responds normally:
4. Throttle – slowly advance to minimum for safe flight
5. Minimize throttle movements
  • Indications: audible bang/pops, EGT ↑, RPM ↓ (FF same) (EP1 #2, p.3, p.60 #17, p.61).
  • EGT/RPM light: EGT > 650 ± 8°C or N1 > 112.4 ± 1% (p.3, p.37, p.54 #121).
  • Locked in stall: EGT over 450°C for 6 seconds after selecting idle (p.54 #120). Matches IC step 3.
  • "3-4 questions … COMP Stall and EGT RPM Light. Know the first 3 steps… 3-4 free questions" (p.5 #20, EP1 #20).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS "Engine Stall = Throttle Idle, Controls Neutralize, EGT/RPM Monitor" (p.59 #2, p.64 #7); "Compressor stall – IDLE, Neutralize, monitor" (EP2 #18); "First step in the EGT/RPM warning light: Neutral/IDLE" (EP1 #24). The current procedure has no "controls neutralize" step: 1. Throttle – idle 2. EGT/RPM – check.

8. Fire in flight

Fire Warning Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — FIRE WARNING LIGHT — MEMORIZE
GROUND
1. Execute emergency shutdown / egress
TAKEOFF
If decision is made to stop:
1. Abort
If fire is confirmed and unable to abort:
2. Eject
IN-FLIGHT
1. Throttle – minimum for safe flight
2. Check for secondary indications of fire
If fire confirmed or flight control lost:
3. Eject
If fire not confirmed and control effectiveness remains:
4. Land as soon as possible
  • Secondary indications of fire: EGT ↑, fuel flow ↑, FIRE light. "All of the above" (p.65, p.47, p.59 #13).
  • FIRE light = firewire > 300°C outside the engine container. It goes out once the firewire stays below 300°C for 45 s (p.37, p.54). If the light comes on then goes out: temp below 300 or burn-through (p.3); check light continuity (p.53 #113).
  • "While cruising 23K you get FIRE light, with 2ndaries – EJECT (not prepare for controlled ejection)" (p.5 #18, EP1 #18).
  • Do not airstart with a fire (p.61, p.65).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.76 #31 Engine Fire in Flight: "a. Throttle – Idle (min for safe flight) b. Secondary Indications – Check / If secondary indications exist – c. Emergency Oxygen Actuator – Pull d. Throttle – Off e. Fuel Shutoff Handle – Pull / If secondary indications persist – f. EJECT / If fire is extinguished – g. Prepare for controlled ejection / If unable to eject – h. Prepare for Flameout Approach". EP2 #36: "Idle, instruments check, secondary-eject". Current: min for safe flight → check secondaries → eject if confirmed or flight control lost → otherwise land as soon as possible.

9. Oil press, ECA 2, ACCEL, LP PUMP, F PRESS

Oil Press Warning Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — OIL PRESS WARNING LIGHT — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle – Set and maintain 78 to 87% rpm.
2. Minimize throttle movements.
  • The OIL PRESS light goes out at about 18% (18–20%) on start (p.56 #170, p.60 #30). Caution threshold: oil pressure differential below 10 psi (p.37).

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS "Power setting for OIL PRESS light = min for safe flight" with handwritten "MINIMIZE THROTTLE MVMTS" (p.59 #5); "Power setting for oil press light: idle." (p.64 #10). Current: 78 to 87% rpm, minimize throttle movements.

Other engine cautions (gouge; not IC 21/43 immediate actions)

  • ECA 2 caution: land as soon as practical; do not exceed 90% above 20K MSL, 95% below 20K MSL, 600°C EGT (EP1 #7, p.65). One ECA lane lost → ECA advisory light (p.47). ECA failure with full trim: RPM/EGT/FF decrease. With no trim: they increase (p.47).
  • ACCEL caution descending through 7,000 MSL: expect slower engine acceleration on the approach (EP1 #3). Cause: steam ingestion bleed valve not in the commanded position (~10% thrust loss if stuck open) or initial shot-of-fuel solenoid failure (p.54 #115, p.38).
  • LP PUMP light in flight: avoid abrupt throttle movements, set minimum for flight (EP1 #10). Illuminates below 35 psi (p.37).
  • F PRESS light, after checking the engine switch on: avoid negative-G maneuvers (EP1 #17). F PRESS also comes on 30 s after a GEN failure (boost pumps shed) (p.39, p.61).
  • FUEL LOW ≈ 350 lbs (p.39, p.47).

10. Electrical

Electrical Fire

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — ELECTRICAL FIRE — MEMORIZE
1. Gen switch - off
  • EP2 #3: "Electrical fire immediate action – GEN switch off". Consistent.

Total Electrical Failure

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — TOTAL ELECTRICAL FAILURE — MEMORIZE
1. Emergency oxygen green ring(s) - pull

Gouge follow-on steps (EP 11X, p.68/p.74; not memory items): Emergency Oxygen Actuator – Pull · Proceed to VMC as soon as possible · Land as soon as practical · Attempt to reset generator / check batteries on · Landing gear – emergency extend · Plan a no-flap/no-slat approach and arrested landing, if available. A short-field trap is recommended because there is no NWS. - No load shedding ("you can eliminate three of the four answers because they include load shedding") (p.74, p.71 #28, EP2 #26). - No emergency flaps with total electrical failure (p.3). Only the gear can be extended manually (p.51 #52). Approach idle engages (p.55 #163). AC/pressurization stay on (PRSOV fails open) (p.44).

Generator failure (gouge; not an IC 21/43 immediate action)

  1. Confirm engine operation · 2. GEN switch – reset · 3. Voltmeter – check · If it won't reset or voltage is abnormal: Battery switches – check on · Generator switch – off · Unnecessary electrical equipment – secure · Proceed to VMC · Land as soon as practical · If MFDs are required: Display Power – ORIDE (Norm when no longer needed) · If it resets with normal voltage: continue (p.70, p.75 #15).
  2. First thing: confirm engine operation (p.71 #16, EP2 #30). Reset momentarily; don't hold it in reset (p.67).
  3. Indications: master caution, tone, GEN warning light, loss of right MFD and HUD (p.3, p.52 #72). The left MFD reverts to ADI and goes out after 2 min unless ORIDE (p.51 #58). F PRESS comes on after 30 s and CAUG comes on (p.51).
  4. Kept: TACAN, GINA, primary stab trim only. Lost: aileron, rudder, and standby stab trim, trim indicators, HUD, right MFD (p.4, p.55, p.8 #35, p.76 #25). Batteries: 27 min (two) / 12 min (one) (p.51 #62).
  5. Overvoltage trips the generator at 30.2 VDC. Undervoltage deenergizes non-essential buses below 25 VDC (p.47, p.51).

AC inverters

  • One inverter: AC INV caution + tone; lose non-essential AC (VOR/ILS/MB/RADALT); keep TACAN azimuth (p.52 #64, #67, #69).
  • Two inverters: all AC lost; PAR approaches only (p.52 #65, #68).
  • AC INV light: reset No. 1 and No. 2 inverters (EP2 #32).

11. Oxygen and ECS

Adverse Physiological Symptoms

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — ADVERSE PHYSIOLOGICAL SYMPTOMS — MEMORIZE
If above 10,000 feet cabin altitude:
1. Emergency oxygen green ring(s) - pull
2. OBOGS flow selector(s) – off
3. Descend below 10,000 feet cabin altitude.

Rapid Decompression

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — RAPID DECOMPRESSION — MEMORIZE
1. Emergency oxygen green ring(s) - pull
2. OBOGS flow selector(s) – off
3. Descend below 10,000 feet cabin altitude.

Oxygen Warning Light

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — OXYGEN WARNING LIGHT — MEMORIZE
IN-FLIGHT
1. Throttle - Set minimum 80% rpm.
2. Execute Adverse Physiological Symptoms (as required).
  • OXYGEN light causes: O2 concentration < 95%, overheat > 250°F, or OXYGEN switch off. OBOGS still works just below 95% (p.53 #106, p.51, p.44).
  • If OBOGS won't reset: leave OBOGS off and use the green apple, so bad OBOGS O2 doesn't contaminate the emergency O2 (p.66 #26, p.70 #14, p.75 #14).
  • Emergency O2 bottle: 1800–2500 psi, lasts 4–20 min, and can be shut off and restarted (p.45).
  • Descend below 10,000 ft cabin altitude with an OBOGS failure (EP2 #1, p.7 #1). This matches the IC 21/43 physiological / decompression altitude.

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS "OXYGEN warning light, system won't reset – set OBOGS/ANTI-G switch off" (p.7 #8, EP2 #8); "OBOGS Contamination (OXYGEN Warning Light): descend to less than 10,000 CABIN ALT or Minimum Safe Altitude" (p.68 #7, p.75 #19). Current: Throttle – set minimum 80% rpm, then execute Adverse Physiological Symptoms as required (green ring(s) – pull, OBOGS flow selector(s) – off, descend below 10,000 ft cabin altitude).

CABIN ALT warning light (gouge; not on the IC 21/43 list)

EP 11X (p.68 #1, p.74 #1, p.64 #1): a. OBOGS – on and mask tight · b. Reduce AOA (if req) · c. AIR FLOW KNOB – off then on · If the warning light remains on: d. Altitude – descend below 25,000 ft MSL · e. Land as soon as practical. - Why 25K and not 10K: "CABIN PRESSURE OR A/C FAILURE DOESN'T MEAN YOU LOSE O2" (p.68, p.70 #10). EP2 #17: "Cabin pressurization failure – descend below 25K MSL". - The light comes on at a cabin altitude above 24,500 ± 500 ft, or for a CAU compressor overpressure/overheat (p.44, p.53 #105). - Don't mix this up with IC 21/43 RAPID DECOMPRESSION (descend below 10,000 ft cabin altitude).

Smoke or Fumes in Cockpit

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — SMOKE OR FUMES IN COCKPIT — MEMORIZE
1. Mask – on/tight
2. Initiate controlled descent to below 18,000 feet MSL
3. Air Flow Knob – Off (below 18,000 feet MSL if possible)
If unable to clear smoke or unable to see:
4. Airspeed – reduce (as practical)
5. Warn other cockpit occupant / secure loose items
6. Seat - lower
7. MDC Firing Handle - pull

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.70 #12 / p.75 #13 / EP2 #5: "a. Altitude – Descend below 25,000' (if practical) b. Air Flow Know – Off / If unable to clear smoke or unable to see – c. Airspeed – Reduce d. Warn other cockpit / Secure loose items e. Seat – Lower f. Visor – Down g. MDC Firing Handle – Pull h. Land as soon as possible". Current: Mask – on/tight first, descend below 18,000 ft MSL, no visor or land step in the immediate actions.

Canopy

  • Canopy loss: descend to 10,000 ft or minimum safe altitude and slow below 200 KIAS (p.64 #15, p.68 #6, p.74 #8).
  • CANOPY light in flight: airspeed below 200 kts (EP2 #21). CANOPY light + tone when the canopy is not latched and locked and power is above 95% (p.4, p.53).
  • If the MDC fails, the seat canopy penetrators still fracture the canopy (p.53 #107). Structural damage may require you to blow the canopy (p.60 #32).

12. Hydraulics (gouge; not IC 21/43 immediate actions)

  • HYD 1 or HYD 2 failure: first consideration is to reduce airspeed below 300 KIAS / 0.6 Mach (EP1 #16).
  • HYD 2 failure: monitor the HYD 1 pressure gauge, not the accumulator (EP1 #21, p.59 #15, p.65). The RAT maintains HYD 2 at 2500–3000 psi (EP1 #4, p.62 A).
  • HYD 1 failure: emergency gear, emergency flaps, and an arrested landing are all available ("all of the above"). General services are lost (p.59 #14, p.65). The hook can be lowered but not raised (p.51 #53, p.54 #138).
  • Both HYD 1 and HYD 2 failed with the RAT working but the aircraft "uncontrollable" → EJECT (p.59 #1, p.64 #6).
  • During ACM, HYD caution + RAT caution, then the HYD light goes out → Reset HYD 2 (correct, though not the first thing to do) (p.6 #23, EP1 #23).
Number Meaning (gouge p.33, p.40, p.48, p.49, p.54, p.57)
3000 psi Normal, both systems
1660 ± 110 psi HYD 1 / HYD 2 PRESS caution light on
2000 psi HYD 2 caution light off
1500 psi RAT deploys (HYD 2); HYD 1 priority valve isolates flight controls (general services lost)
1600 psi Priority valve reopens to general services
1800 psi HYD 2 reset / RAT retract allowed
2500–3000 psi Normal RAT output
700 psi RAT retracts as HYD 2 is lost (airloads permitting)
2200 psi Brake/emergency flap accumulator isolates the flaps (brakes only)
10 Brake applications from the accumulator (plus emergency flap extension, no slats)
1100 ± 50 / 1300 ± 50 psi Flight-control accumulator / brake-flap accumulator nitrogen preload
HYD FAIL warning HYD 1 < 600 ± 50 and HYD 2 < 1660 ± 110 and emergency system < 600 ± 50
45% / 42% N2 HYD 2 RESET usable above 45%; bypass valve open below 42%
  • General services lost below 1500 psi HYD 1: normal gear, normal flaps/slats, normal brakes, NWS, L-bar extension, speed brakes, hook retract (p.49 #37).

13. Flight controls

Asymmetric Flaps/Slats

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — ASYMMETRIC FLAPS/SLATS — MEMORIZE
1. FLAPS/SLATS lever – Return to previous setting
  • "Uncommanded roll or yaw when transitioning flaps/slats – return flaps to previous position" (EP2 #7). A rapid roll during flap/slat transition = split flap/slat (p.4).
  • Split slat: uncommanded roll + SLAT caution light. The flap position light may not show a split flap because it is wired to the left flap only (p.35).

SLATS caution light (gouge)

  • Coming into the break with a SLATS light: slow to and maintain below 200 KIAS and land as soon as practical (EP2 #4, p.64 #5, p.71 #30, p.76 #28).
  • Comes on when the slats are not in the selected position, slats are split, or slats are selected or extended above 217 kts (p.42, p.49 #38, p.71 #29).

CAUG caution light (gouge)

  • You lose ARI, SBI, yaw damper, and rudder trim, all four (EP2 #15, p.66 #32, p.71 #19, p.75 #18).
  • It comes on for a system failure, a BIT in progress, or the paddle switch being depressed (p.41, p.61). CAUG BIT needs WOW, < 80 kts, flaps/slats up (p.41, p.52 #86).
  • Trim-failure question → "ADI indicates 2° nose up" (p.64 #3, p.68 #3). A 2° ADI pitch change during speed brake extension/retraction is normal with the SBI (p.67, p.71 #27). The SBI does not change stick position (p.71 #20).
  • Speed brakes may not fully extend above 340 KIAS. Blowback starts above 380 KIAS (p.71, p.67).

14. Brakes, directional control, blown tires, arrestments

Brake Failure – Ashore / Skid

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — BRAKE FAILURE – ASHORE / SKID — MEMORIZE
If flyaway airspeed available:
1. Go around
If flyaway airspeed not available, or during taxi:
2. Throttle - idle
3. Brakes - release
4. Anti-skid switch - off
5. Brakes – apply gradually
If brakes still unavailable:
6. Hook – down (if required)
7. Parking brake handle – pull (if required)

OLDER GOUGE — DIFFERS p.68 #5 Wheel Brake Failure on Touchdown: "Go around, if able / If go around not feasible – Wheel brakes – release / ANTI-SKID – OFF / Attempt braking / Hook – DOWN (1,000' prior to gear) / Throttle – OFF (if departing Rwy) / If airborne – ANTI-SKID – OFF / Attempt a short field arrestment". EP2 #31 is similar. Current: Throttle – idle is the first step after go-around, brakes are applied gradually, and it ends with Parking brake handle – pull (if required).

Brake Failure – Afloat

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — BRAKE FAILURE – AFLOAT — MEMORIZE
1. Throttle - idle
2. Parking brake handle - pull
3. Hook – down
4. Transmit
  • Not covered by the gouge (a topic title only, p.69).

Loss of Directional Control

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — LOSS OF DIRECTIONAL CONTROL — MEMORIZE
If flyaway airspeed available:
1. Go-around
If flyaway airspeed not available:
2. Abort
If blown tire suspected:
3. Brakes - release
4. Anti-skid switch - off
5. Brakes – apply gradually
If NWS failure suspected:
6. Paddle switch - press
  • Gouge: a NWS malfunction is steered with differential braking (EP1 #5, p.59 #10). IC 21/43 adds: if NWS failure is suspected, Paddle switch – press.
  • NWS AUG on during taxi: momentarily press the NWS button (EP1 #25). EMERG gear extension disables NWS (p.3, p.69).

Blown tires (gouge)

  • On T/O (aborting): rudder as required to counter the swerve; ANTI-SKID – OFF prior to braking (p.70 #11, p.76 #21, EP2 #19). Continuing the takeoff: do NOT reposition gear and flaps, monitor engine instruments, then execute the landing-with-blown-tire procedure (p.70).
  • After touchdown: rudder (counter swerve), go around if able (EP2 #9).
  • Single blown tire, no arresting gear: flared landing, flaps ½ (p.73 #31, p.76 #22, EP2 #20).
  • Dual blown tires, no arresting gear: consider a gear-up landing or controlled ejection (p.73 #32, p.67).

Arrestments (gouge)

  • Short field: touch down 500 ft prior to the gear. If directional control is in question: at or 50 ft prior (p.73 #33, p.74 #3, EP2 #6). NWS AUG failure: touch down just before (50 ft) the gear (p.72).
  • Long field (for test): hook down 1,000 ft prior, maintain directional control, release brakes as you cross the cable (p.71 #26).

Anti-skid, hook, gear lights (gouge)

  • Anti-skid failure: release brakes, anti-skid off, brakes as required (EP2 #34, p.76 #29).
  • Anti-skid OFF, 5 cases: after HYD 1 failure, before setting the parking brake after shutdown, carrier landings, SKID caution light, blown tire (p.3, p.35). With one switch off, the system is off (p.49).
  • HOOK light: hook position doesn't agree with the handle ("not fully extended") (p.71 #17, EP2 #33).
  • Gear handle light: gear in transit or not in the commanded position (p.71, p.75, p.76).
  • WHEELS warning light: gear handle not DOWN, N2 < 95%, and either < 7,200 ft MSL with < 170 kts, or FLAPS/SLATS not up (p.42).

15. Departure / spin

Departure/Spin Procedure

IC 21/43 (Nov 2023) — DEPARTURE/SPIN PROCEDURE — MEMORIZE
1. Controls – neutralize / forcibly center rudder pedals
2. Speed Brakes - retract
3. Throttle - idle
4. Check altitude, AOA, Airspeed, and turn needle
If spin confirmed:
INVERTED (AOA pegged at 0 units):
5. Rudder pedal – full opposite turn needle
6. Lateral stick – full opposite turn needle
7. Longitudinal stick - neutralize
UPRIGHT (AOA above 28 units):
8. Rudder pedal – full opposite turn needle
9. Lateral stick – full with turn needle
10. Longitudinal stick - neutralize
If recovery indicated or airspeed increasing through 160 KIAS:
11. Lateral stick - neutralize
When recovery indicated:
12. Rudder pedals – smoothly center
If out of control passing through 10,000 feet AGL:
13. Eject
  • Gouge aero items, all consistent with the current procedure: rudder full opposite turn needle for both upright and inverted. Aileron with the turn needle when upright, opposite when inverted. "Aileron positions reversed for spin recovery" (p.20 #43–45, p.25, p.1 #8/#12).
  • Inverted spin: 0 units indicated AOA, negative G, turn needle in the direction of the spin (p.2 #40, p.20 #44). True AOA −60/−40/−25 with airspeeds 50–120 / 100–160 / 140–200 and descent rates 15–20,000 / 15–20,000 / 11,000 fpm (p.25).
  • Upright spin at 45° AOA: about 15,000 fpm descent (p.1 #17). An upright spin is NOT characterized by recovery AOA increasing past 5 units (p.2 #28).

16. Avionics and instrument failures (gouge)

  • MFD failure: blank or blinking screen, stuck pushbutton, wrong/invalid info (EP2 #11, p.74 #6). One MFD: turn it off. Multiple MFDs: Display Power switch momentarily to RESET (EP2 #22, p.47 #10, p.76 #27).
  • DEU failure: MFDs and HUD lost, PAR (or visual) only (p.30, p.52 #88, p.55 #146).
  • GINA failure: "all the above" (EP2 #10). Total GINA failure blanks attitude and heading on the ADI, HSI, and HUD (p.15).
  • Pitot-static failure: use the standby VSI. You lose mach/airspeed and baro altitude, NOT vertical velocity (EP2 #27, p.14).
  • Barometric altimeter failure: RADALT below 5,000 ft AGL, cabin altimeter if unpressurized, check pitot heat (EP2 #12, p.76 #24).
  • ADI failure: standby attitude indicator. AOA failure: use airspeed (p.15, p.31).
  • IFF failure: ATC reports loss of altitude info (p.32).

18. Miscellaneous exam gouge

  • Midair: have someone else check you out (not the guy you just hit). Don't have the wingman join up (p.59 #4, p.64 #9, p.65).
  • In ejection situations, trade airspeed for altitude (p.60 #27). If flight controls are uncontrollable → eject (p.65).
  • Increase altitude for better radio reception ("really… it's on there") (p.60 #31).
  • "Don't worry about PA procedures" (p.64 #18). "MEMORIZE ① FIRE ② GTS FIRE ③ GENERATOR FAILURE" (p.66). "KNOW THE BOLD FACE" (p.65).
