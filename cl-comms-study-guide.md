# Comm flow — ordered call/response script (verbatim)

## Comms 1. Clearance, Base/Strike & Ground

1. **— → ATIS** [Ch 30 Pri] — Pri Radio - On, Channel 30 (ATIS) — When done listening, switch to ch 29, turn off, put altimeter setting in  _(Doc)_
2. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “ICS check, switches set, Norm, Fuel __”  _(Doc)_
3. **Rear cockpit (R/C) → Pilot (front)** [ICS] — “Loud and Clear, switches set, Norm, Both, Fuel __”  _(Doc)_
4. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Lights and Tones”  _(Doc)_
5. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Throttle off, GTS”  _(Doc)_
6. **Rear cockpit (R/C) → Pilot (front)** [ICS] — “Clear”  _(Doc)_
7. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “No Exceedances, No Overflows, 4 SATS, QUALS Counting Down”  _(Doc)_
8. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Mask on, Flow on”  _(Doc)_
9. **Pilot → Kingsville Clearance** [Ch 29 Pri] — Kingsville Clearance, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX (Premont 2 on Request, Ready to Copy)  _(Comm Script)_
10. **Kingsville Clearance → Pilot** [Ch 29 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX you are cleared to Kingsville via the KINGS 3 Departure RICTO transition, Premont 2 coded route, on departure climb and maintain 7 thousand expect 230 in 10, SQUAWK 513X, Departure Local button 4.  _(Comm Script)_
11. **Pilot → Kingsville Clearance** [Ch 29 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX is cleared to Kingsville via the KINGS 3 Departure RICTO transition, Premont 2 coded route, on departure climb and maintain 7 thousand expect 230 in 10, SQUAWK 513X, Departure Local button 4.  _(Comm Script)_
12. **Kingsville Clearance → Pilot** [Ch 29 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX read back correct, frequency change approved.  _(Comm Script)_
13. **Pilot → Kingsville Clearance** [Ch 29 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Switching  _(Comm Script)_
14. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Good tone, Good BIT”  _(Doc)_
15. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Watch your knees”  _(Doc)_
16. **Rear cockpit (R/C) → Pilot (front)** [ICS] — “Clear”  _(Doc)_
17. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Canopy”  _(Doc)_
18. **Rear cockpit (R/C) → Pilot (front)** [ICS] — “Rail Clear”  _(Doc)_
19. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Altimeter Set __.__, indicating ___ on standby, ___ on glass. VOR Set ___.__, TACAN Set ___, IFF Set XXXX in Standby, Taxi Checks Complete”  _(Doc)_
20. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Attached 8 Points, Pins Removed, Ready to Arm in Front”  _(Doc)_
21. **Rear cockpit (R/C) → Pilot (front)** [ICS] — “Attached 8 Points, Pins Removed, Armed in Back”  _(Doc)_
22. **Pilot (front) → Rear cockpit (R/C)** [ICS] — “Armed in Front, Takeoff Checks Complete”  _(Doc)_
23. **Pilot → STRIKE (Base)** [Ch 1 Aux] — STRIKE, 2XX out of chocks.  _(Comm Script)_
24. **STRIKE (Base) → Pilot** [Ch 1 Aux] — 2XX have a good flight.  _(Comm Script)_
25. **Pilot → Base** [Ch 1 Aux] — “Base, 01 Taxi” (You just say the last two numbers)  _(Doc)_
26. **Base → Pilot** [Ch 1 Aux] — “201 have a good flight”  _(Doc)_
27. **Pilot → Kingsville Ground** [Ch 2 Pri] — Kingsville Ground, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, taxi with ALPHA (current ATIS).  _(Comm Script)_
28. **Kingsville Ground → Pilot** [Ch 2 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX taxi to 13R via Bravo Alpha.  _(Comm Script)_
29. **Pilot → Kingsville Ground** [Ch 2 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX taxi to 13R via Bravo Alpha.  _(Comm Script)_

1. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — Kingsville Approach, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX XX Thousand with ATIS, request. — Doc: “Kingsville Approach, Blazer 201 one five thousand with information bravo, flash request” Hit IDENT after saying flash request.  _(Comm Script + Doc)_
2. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Kingsville Approach, IDENT.  _(Comm Script)_
3. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Radar Contact, 8 miles West of Kleberg County 9 thousand 1 hundred, say request.  _(Comm Script)_
4. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — Kingsville Approach, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX request HI-TCN 13R. — Doc: “Request the HI-TACAN 13R, Blazer 201”  _(Comm Script + Doc)_
5. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, proceed direct WADE, climb and maintain 15 thousand, Kingsville altimeter 29.92  _(Comm Script)_
6. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, direct WAADE, climbing to 15 thousand, 29.92  _(Comm Script)_
7. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, cleared HI-TCN 13R, report commencing.  _(Comm Script)_

## Comms 5. VFR Initial, Break & Landing Pattern

1. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — Kingsville Approach, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Cancel VFR to the Initial. (Or Vectors to the Initial.)  _(Comm Script)_
2. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX proceed to the initial for the 13’s, cancelation received, maintain this frequency for advisories, Kingsville altimeter 29.92.  _(Comm Script)_
3. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX WILCO 29.92.  _(Comm Script)_
4. **Kingsville Approach → Pilot** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX switch Kingsville Arrival button 12.  _(Comm Script)_
5. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach** [Ch 13 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX switching 12.  _(Comm Script)_
6. **Pilot → Kingsville Arrival** [Ch 12 Pri] — Kingsville Arrival, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX checking in 4 thousand 5 hundred.  _(Comm Script)_
7. **Kingsville Arrival → Pilot** [Ch 12 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, Kingsville Arrival (traffic advisories if any).  _(Comm Script)_
8. **Kingsville Arrival → Pilot** [Ch 12 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX no traffic between you and the initial squawk standby and contact tower.  _(Comm Script)_
9. **Pilot → Kingsville Arrival** [Ch 12 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX squawking standby switching.  _(Comm Script)_
10. **Kingsville Approach/Arrival → Pilot** [IFR to initial] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX fly heading 040 descend and maintain 4 thousand, report Kingsville in sight, Kingsville altimeter 29.92.  _(Comm Script)_
11. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach/Arrival** [IFR to initial] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, 040 down to 4 thousand, vectors for the initial, WILCO, Kingsville altimeter 29.92.  _(Comm Script)_
12. **Pilot → Kingsville Approach/Arrival** [IFR to initial] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX field in sight.  _(Comm Script)_
13. **Kingsville Approach/Arrival → Pilot** [IFR to initial] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX no traffic between you and the initial squawk standby and contact tower. → BLAZR/HAWK 2XX squawking standby switching.  _(Comm Script)_
14. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — Kingsville Tower, BLAZR/HAWK 2XX 15 miles to the West for the initial.  _(Comm Script)_
15. **Kingsville Tower → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX report the initial for the 13’s  _(Comm Script)_
16. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, WILCO.  _(Comm Script)_
17. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Initial.  _(Comm Script)_
18. **Kingsville Tower → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX report the numbers.  _(Comm Script)_
19. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, numbers.  _(Comm Script)_
20. **Kingsville Tower → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX left break approved.  _(Comm Script)_
21. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX abeam, gear, (option, touch and go, full stop).  _(Comm Script)_
22. **Kingsville Tower → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX, winds 130 at 15 cleared option 13L.  _(Comm Script)_
23. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX cleared option 13L.  _(Comm Script)_
24. **Pilot → Tower/LSO** [Ch 3 Pri] — 2XX Goshawk Ball, fuel state. (ICS no FCLP’s) — Or CLARA **Without Ball**  _(Comm Script)_
25. **Tower/LSO → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — Roger Ball  _(Comm Script)_
26. **Kingsville Tower → Pilot** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX your interval is abeam mid field, left downwind approved. Or BLAZR/HAWK 2XX left closed traffic is approved. Or BLAZR/HAWK 2XX maintain your interval.  _(Comm Script)_
27. **Pilot → Kingsville Tower** [Ch 3 Pri] — BLAZR/HAWK 2XX interval in sight (or BLAZR/HAWK 2XX Roger.)  _(Comm Script)_
