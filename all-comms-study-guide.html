Source conflicts / variants

  • Ch 5 (Kings 1A) frequency: Comm Script “Ch 5 (237.85)” vs Checklist Gouge “On CH5, it should read 238.75”. Verify against current local pubs.
  • MOA 99 call: Script “99 in the Alpha check in” · Checklist Gouge “99, anyone working 1A?” · Doc “99, BI single claims 1A-NE” vs Gouge “99, BI flight of one claims 1A-NE.” Checkout: Doc “…9 thousand, RTB Kingsville” vs Gouge “…proceeding direct WAADE”.
  • Arrival channel after commencing: Script IFR “local channel 14 / switching 14”; Doc “channel 12 / switching one two”; Script VFR uses button 12.
  • Clearance: Script “expect 230 in 10, SQUAWK 513X, Departure Local button 4” vs Gouge “expect higher in 10, departure local button 4, squawk 513X” (order differs). Doc Departure: “climb and maintain ten thousand”.
  • Strike/Base: Script/Gouge “Strike, 2XX out of chocks” vs Doc “Base, 01 taxi” vs Video “Base 01, out of chocks”. After landing: Doc “Base, 01 on deck” / Gouge “Strike, XXX, back and (up/down)” (at taxi-back checklist) / Video “Base, 01, safe on deck”. Doc calls Base+Ground before leaving Marshall; Gouge calls Ground before leaving Marshall and Strike “during taxi”.
  • Tower takeoff reply: Script “change to departure” vs Gouge “switch to departure”; readback Gouge “cleared for takeoff runway 13R, switching”.
  • Lineup: Doc “Good headings, no off flags, watch your knees” vs Gouge “…knees.” Takeoff roll gear/flaps: Doc “140, gear, flaps” vs Gouge “Gear”/“140, flaps” or “Above 140, gear, flaps”; Gouge alt “Gear and flaps up prior to 200, good handle.”
  • OBOGS: Doc “Mask on, Flow on” vs Gouge “Flow test. Mask on in the front.” → “Mask on in the rear.” vs Video “Mask on, flow on in the front. OBOGS integrity check.”
  • Canopy reply: Doc/Gouge “Rail clear” vs Video “Clear”.
  • Takeoff checklist ICS: Gouge reads the whole placard aloud (“CAUG set ALL, light extinguished. Anti-Skid ON… Harness unlocked, attached eight points, pins removed, ready to arm in the front.”); Doc/Video only the seat line.
  • Approach: Gouge reply “go ahead” and clearance “climb and maintain one-five thousand, proceed direct WAADE, report commencing” (one transmission) vs Script IDENT → radar contact → say request → direct WADE … → cleared HI-TCN 13R report commencing. Spelling WADE/WAADE.
  • Shutdown: Doc “Throttle Off, Canopy” vs Gouge “All equipment off, clear OBOGS and canopy. Throttle off canopy.”; Taxi back Doc “No exceedances, no overflows” vs Gouge/Video “…Good jet in the front.” → “Good jet in the back.”
  • FAF call: Doc labels it Ch 14 Pri to Tower; Script makes it after Tower’s “how do you hear?” on the Arrival freq.
  • McCain video is a different base: KARP callsign, UNION 1 departure, squawk 4230, Maintenance “in and up / park Bravo”. NAV0102 slide reads “MAY, MAY, MAYDAY” (standard is MAYDAY ×3).

Phraseology, frequencies & lost-comms quick reference

Kingsville channels

  • Ch 30 Pri ATIS (plate 282.25)
  • Ch 29 Pri Clearance (328.4)
  • Ch 2 Pri Ground (239.05)
  • Ch 1 Aux Base/Strike
  • Ch 3 Pri Tower (124.1 / 377.05) — auto-switch abeam arresting gear
  • Ch 4 Pri Departure “button 4” (121.05 / 266.8)
  • Ch 5 Pri Kings 1A 237.85 (Script) / 238.75 (Gouge)
  • Ch 13 Pri Approach (119.9 / 290.45)
  • Ch 14 (IFR, Script) / Ch 12 (VFR & Doc) Arrival
  • Guard 243.0 / 121.5 · FSS 255.4 / 122.2

Phraseology

  • Call-up: Facility, callsign, position/altitude, request
  • Readbacks end/begin with callsign; training = read back all clearances verbatim; real world = runway & hold-short at minimum
  • Refuse an unacceptable clearance & state reasons — readback = acceptance
  • Clearance elements (NAV0102, CRAFT-style): ID, Clearance limit, Route/SID, Altitude, Frequency (departure), Transponder (squawk), + holding. (CRAFT itself isn’t named in the sources; mapping shown.)
  • Base/Strike: last two digits only (“01”)
  • “Wilco” = will comply; “switching” on frequency changes; IDENT on initial Approach call (“flash”)
  • Report entering/leaving holding (except mil terminal + radar + training); RVSM level-off; “executing missed approach”

Lost comms (NAV0102 / Gouge)

  • Squawk 7600 (all weather); try other freqs & Guard 243.0/121.5
  • VMC: stay VMC, land ASAP practicable; light signals; rock wings/flash lights
  • IMC route AVEF: Assigned → Vectored → Expected → Filed
  • IMC altitude AME: highest of Assigned / Minimum IFR (MEA) / Expected
  • Leave clearance limit per EFC, or ETA (T/O + ETE)
  • GCA pattern ≤25 NM IMC: higher of last assigned or MSA until established
  • NORDO to alternate: 7600, published MAP, proceed as filed
  • Emergency: 7700, MAYDAY×3 (distress) / PAN-PAN×3 (urgency) + station, ID/type, nature, wx, intentions, position, altitude, fuel, souls
