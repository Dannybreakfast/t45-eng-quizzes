Comm Flow Study Guide — Call & Response Script

Radio and ICS calls only, in order, verbatim. Blue = you (front cockpit), amber = rear cockpit, green = ATC/Base/other. Comms knowledge pack

Comms 1. Clearance, Base/Strike & Ground · Comms 2. Tower, Takeoff ICS & Departure · Comms 3. MOA (Ch 5) & Enroute ICS · Comms 4. Recovery — Approach, Arrival & Tower · Comms 5. VFR Initial, Break & Landing Pattern · Comms 6. Landing ICS, After Landing & Shutdown

Source conflicts

  • Arrival channel after “commencing”: Comm Script IFR RECOVERY: “local channel 14” / “switching 14”; Doc: “local channel 12” / “Switching one two”. (VFR-to-initial uses button 12 in the Script.)
  • Arrival check-in altitude: Doc IFR: “one four thousand 500 hundred”; Script VFR: “4 thousand 5 hundred”; Script IFR: “XX thousand request touch-n-go, climb out for GCA Box”.
  • Clearance altitude: Script: “climb and maintain 7 thousand expect 230 in 10”; Doc Departure: “climb and maintain ten thousand”.
  • FAF call: Doc labels it “Ch 14 Pri: Kingsville Tower, Blazer 201, FAF with gear”; Script makes it after Tower’s “how do you hear?” on the Arrival frequency.
  • Callsign / Base: Doc: “Blazer 201”, “Base, 01 Taxi”; Script: “BLAZR/HAWK 2XX”, “STRIKE, 2XX out of chocks.”
  • Tower takeoff call: Script “take off.”; Doc “takeoff”.
  • Video (ASR, reference only) narrates the Ground call as “taxi, golf” and Departure as “passing 900 for 7000”; not used for answers.
